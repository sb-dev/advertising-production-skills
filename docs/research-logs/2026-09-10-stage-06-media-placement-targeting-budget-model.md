| Owner | Owns | Does not obtain merely by owning that work |

| Advertising Production | Campaign channel/placement roles inside that strategy, audience hypotheses, creative/media fit, staged allocation proposals, test feasibility, measurement interpretation and bounded repair | Authority to change commercial inputs, collect private data, grant legal clearance, purchase inventory or mutate accounts |
| Authorised execution systems and operators | Account administration, purchases, uploads, bid/budget settings, serving and technical evidence for explicitly authorised operations | Authority to redefine strategy or count their own successful API call as domain, legal or business acceptance |

A campaign media plan is a versioned, readable record in the consuming project. It may be one Markdown file with lines and links; no DSP, account store, CRM, universal API, scheduler or manually maintained graph is introduced. Reuse Stage 3's source, knowledge, validity, use-decision and `real`/`synthetic` conventions. Reuse Stage 5's requirement-profile classification. Do not create another approval-state vocabulary.

The header identifies plan/revision, owner, brief/business baseline, objective, authorised channel class, campaign period, cost basis/currency, requested next decision and unresolved dependencies. Each media line has an ID/revision and links to the following complete content:

| Line content | Required relationship |
|---|---|
| Strategic role | M01 channel, M02 placement and the objective each should advance |
| Audience and controls | M03 hypothesis and exact Stage 3 segment/representation; M04 geography; M10 exclusions |
| Timing and exposure | M05 schedule, M06 frequency and M07 reach, with units, windows and evidence status |
| Delivery and learning intent | M08 optimisation mapping, M09 test/phase role and Stage 8 experiment dependency |

| Feasibility and acceptance | Current capability evidence, control limitations, actual configuration/return required, blockers, owner and permitted next commitment |

A media line can reference existing records instead of duplicating them. It cannot hide missing information behind a label or an inaccessible URL. Distinguish a desired rule, documented support, verified account configuration and observed delivery. Stage 3's `enforced`, `signal-only`, `unsupported` and `unverified` classifications remain the mapping vocabulary. An `enforced` label must state the rule's operational meaning and known identity, reporting or coverage limitations; it never means infallible knowledge about a person.

## 5. Eleven required media dimensions

### M01. Channel role

**Record.** Channel ID, parent approved channel strategy, job within the campaign, advertising objective, audience/context rationale, expected contribution path, relevant evidence, alternatives, dependencies and success/guardrail observations. Distinguish capturing expressed demand, building recognition, explaining a proposition and re-engaging an authorised prior audience. These are candidate roles, not mandatory funnel stages or fixed budget proportions.

**Decision and cheap check.** Advertising explains why the role is needed within the approved business strategy. Compare a one-page role/creative/destination map before selecting a buying product. A channel name alone does not establish its job; the same channel can serve different objectives.

**Failure and repair.** Duplicate roles with no learning purpose, a channel dependent on unavailable creative, or a role that rewards the wrong response require a campaign-plan correction. A proposed business-wide channel expansion returns upstream. Preserve the approved offer, segment and evidence; do not solve weak qualification by silently choosing a different customer.

### M02. Placement role

**Record.** Placement ID, parent channel role, named provider/media-owner product and buying mode when selected, inventory/context, device/modality, expected attention and interaction, language, format/profile version, assembly/enhancement controls, destination route and acceptance requirements. Unknown selection is explicit planning state, not an executable placement.

**Decision and cheap check.** Pair the intended creative with the actual context requirement: what can the recipient see, hear and do? Inspect a rough treatment or exact text against the selected profile before commissioning the asset. Keep format, orientation and placement distinct. Auction, reservation and direct publisher arrangements need their own feasibility and commitment information; a channel role does not choose one automatically.

**Failure and repair.** An unclickable context with a click-dependent CTA, an unsupported format or an uncontrolled enhancement blocks that route. Repair the placement treatment, ask for a supported authorised alternative, or return a commercial change to its owner. Do not regenerate the parent campaign idea for a local delivery defect. The TopReach constraint example in S10 demonstrates why product-level checks are needed; it is not a provider selection.

### M03. Audience hypothesis

**Record.** Exact audience-hypothesis and approved business-segment revisions; hypothesised context/need/role and response; supporting and contrary evidence; uncertainty; permitted inclusion/exclusion boundaries; proposed representation; and use/activation authority where data is involved. Targetable platform categories are not a substitute for customer evidence.

**Decision and cheap check.** Advertising may vary a communication hypothesis within the approved scope. Inspect a rule-to-control matrix before activation, including how automated expansion changes the delivered population. A contextual proposal can be evaluated without obtaining individual records. S06 supports treating signals and restrictions separately.

**Failure and repair.** Signal-only treatment of a hard restriction, unsupported sensitive inference, missing data-use authority or delivery outside approved scope blocks the dependent route. Change the authorised mapping or seek the owning decision; do not relabel expansion as an unchanged audience test. Preserve the original segment and historical delivered context. A changed audience is a changed treatment, even when creative is identical.

### M04. Geography

**Record.** Served commercial market, intended recipient geography, geographic selection meaning, inclusions/exclusions, location granularity, language/locale, fulfilment limits, legal-jurisdiction questions, exact provider mapping and evidence/limitations. Presence, residence, interest in a place, language and ability to buy are distinct predicates.

**Decision and cheap check.** Compare the required population with what the proposed control actually represents. Review synthetic boundary cases: a visitor, an interested person outside the market and a recipient in an excluded area. S03 supports the semantic distinction, not a guarantee of precise location. A language translation is not market-entry approval.

**Failure and repair.** A geographic hint cannot satisfy a stricter commercial or legal requirement. An unresolvable mismatch blocks that execution route; no tolerance may be invented. A destination eligibility check can complement permitted media controls, but cannot retroactively authorise prohibited ad exposure. Business/Legal owners resolve scope; the executor corrects configuration. Preserve unaffected media lines.

### M05. Schedule

**Record.** Flight start/end and boundary convention, named timezone, serving days/hours, offer and approval validity, asset/destination readiness, reservation/cancellation deadlines, fulfilment capacity, monitoring windows and separate observation/maturation horizon. Record requested, confirmed and actual dates separately. An executor's timezone and supported scheduling granularity must be verified rather than assumed.

**Decision and cheap check.** Convert a small set of boundary instants, including a daylight-saving transition where relevant, and check the intersection of serving, offer, asset-rights and authority windows. Test cross-midnight interpretation explicitly. S08/S10 show why the plan cannot assume every product supports day-parting. Scheduling is not a budget limiter or a guarantee of impressions.

**Failure and repair.** A flight outside offer validity, impossible readiness dependency or unsupported hard hours blocks dependent commitment. Correct timing or select an authorised compatible route; do not extend a price or rights expiry. A safety pause or early termination is recorded as actual execution only when an authorised operator returns evidence. Preserve valid observations with the dates that actually applied.

### M06. Frequency

**Record.** Intended exposure pattern and rationale, counted event, identity basis, cap/target/average/distribution, period, aggregation level, cross-line or cross-asset overlap, configuration support, reporting method and limitations. Keep a desired repetition pattern, a configured cap and observed frequency separate. There is no default universal optimum or cross-platform person-level guarantee.

**Decision and cheap check.** Inspect whether the requirement concerns impressions, viewable impressions, views, devices, accounts or people, and whether the executor controls that same object. Compare average with available distribution; equal averages can conceal different concentration. S04/S05 establish documented counting and modelling differences, not a particular campaign's exposure.

**Failure and repair.** Incomparable numerator/identity/window, an unsupported cross-campaign cap or an arbitrary fatigue declaration prevents the relevant conclusion or execution claim. Repair the mapping or narrow the claim about control. A new creative ID must not be assumed to reset a person's total exposure history. Frequency can inform fatigue diagnosis later; it is not sufficient evidence of fatigue by itself.

### M07. Reach

**Record.** Intended population, potential eligible audience, forecast reach, observed/modelled reach, counting identity, window, geography, inclusion basis, provider/method, uncertainty and deduplication scope. State which of these is present. A forecast audience size is not purchased unique reach; an impression is not evidence of attention or understanding.

**Decision and cheap check.** Check whether the reported/forecast number describes people, devices, households or another unit. Inspect overlap assumptions before combining lines. In an ideal same-population set model, two exact reaches have union between the larger individual reach and their sum; model uncertainty and incompatible populations prevent treating those bounds as measured results. S05 supports keeping provider reach methodology and delay visible.

**Failure and repair.** Do not sum platform reach into unique people without valid deduplication. Unavailable reach does not equal zero, and a precise dashboard number is not an exact census. Obtain compatible evidence or report separate values and uncertainty. Repair measurement interpretation rather than changing the creative to fit a misleading forecast.

### M08. Optimisation objective

**Record.** Business and advertising objective parents, intended outcome, platform/executor goal when selected, actual event/action set, event definitions, biddable versus observation-only mapping, value/currency basis, latency, attribution/reporting window, qualification and downstream guardrails. A configured target is not an achieved CPA, ROAS or business result.

**Decision and cheap check.** Ask what improving the chosen signal would reward. Inspect goal/action configuration and test-event evidence supplied by its technical owner before making execution recommendations. S07 shows why a field labelled secondary is not necessarily non-biddable in every goal arrangement. Retain an explicit gap between a proxy and retained business value.

**Failure and repair.** A goal that rewards accidental visits, unqualified forms or unverified revenue cannot justify scale. Correct the event or mapping through its owner, or keep the plan at a bounded learning scope with actual authority. Do not manufacture downstream values, assume a platform learning period proves statistical sufficiency, or change the offer to improve the reported metric.

### M09. Test allocation

**Record.** Test/phase ID, decision question, treatment and comparison, changed/fixed dimensions, allocation unit, planned budget/traffic allocation with denominator, audience overlap/interference, time alignment, execution mechanism, cost/volume assumptions, feasibility limits, stop/review conditions and dependency on the complete Stage 8 contract. Allocation percentages must state whether they concern money, traffic, users or inventory.

**Decision and cheap check.** Compare equal, unequal and adaptive allocation against the question and available resources. A simple arithmetic scenario can expose an unaffordable design; it cannot establish power or effectiveness. Preserve a comparison where causal learning is intended. Several ad sets competing for delivery do not automatically constitute a randomised experiment.

**Failure and repair.** Fragmented funding, missing comparison, unexplained adaptive redistribution or concurrent audience/creative changes require a design correction or a more limited interpretation. Reserve cannot be released because one arm has an early better ratio. Stage 8 must supply the full evidence threshold, confounders and decision rule before a real test; separate spending authority remains mandatory. Preserve the actual allocation history, including safety interventions.

### M10. Exclusions

**Record.** Rule ID and exact predicate; reason and owning authority; category such as commercial eligibility, geography, prior action, data use, placement, context or test interference; binding versus preference classification; affected line; current representation/configuration; source freshness; verification evidence; limitations; and expiry/review trigger. Customer suppression data remains with its authorised owner, not in this public repository.

**Decision and cheap check.** Evaluate synthetic allowed/forbidden cases against each rule. Separate exclusions from positive signals and check interactions: an allowed audience may still be excluded by eligibility or context. For time-dependent suppression, identify the update owner and latency before claiming a purchaser cannot be re-exposed. S09 illustrates that inventory filters are not universal audience controls.

**Failure and repair.** Missing authority, stale suppression, unsupported control or an account-level assumption that does not apply to the chosen product blocks the affected path. Narrow to a demonstrably compatible route or escalate; do not silently remove exclusions to increase reach. Overbroad preference filters may be reconsidered by their owner, but hard constraints cannot be traded for cheaper inventory.

### M11. Brand safety

**Record.** Baseline prohibited contexts, brand-specific suitability choices, exact reasons and source/authority, inventory/media-owner scope, treatment of unknown/unclassified inventory, selected controls, coverage limitations, verification source, monitoring responsibility, escalation and authorised response. Keep contextual suitability, viewer eligibility, creative truthfulness, invalid traffic, technical quality and legal compliance independently assessable.

**Decision and cheap check.** Review a bounded context matrix with acceptable, unacceptable and ambiguous cases before purchasing. S11 supplies historical context for the safety/suitability distinction; S09 supplies current product limitations. The project decision is to use reasoned contextual rules rather than blindly exclude broad topics or assume a platform's monetisation policy meets every brand requirement.

**Failure and repair.** A mandatory restriction without sufficient control/verification blocks that route. A preference with residual risk needs the appropriate owner's explicit scoped decision; the skill cannot supply it. Unexpected adjacency requires preserving exact placement/time evidence and requesting the authorised containment and repair. Do not claim zero risk, weaken legal constraints, or fix an inventory problem by rewriting a valid ad. A verification vendor's report is evidence with coverage, not a blanket guarantee.

## 6. Budget model and authorised staging

### B01. Planning envelope, exposure and controls

The budget record extends Stage 3 C07 rather than creating a competing financial truth. Record the upstream envelope/revision; currency and monetary precision; period; included media, production, measurement, fees/tax presentation and other costs as supplied; excluded costs and their separate owner; planning assumptions; proposed line/phase allocations; reserve; incurred cost evidence; non-cancellable liabilities; and exact approvals. Unknown cost is not zero. An estimate, allocation, commitment, served cost, billed cost, credit and cash payment are different facts.

For a planning version, all included allocations plus retained reserve must not exceed the envelope on a consistent basis. For media suballocation, each phase and line must reconcile to its parent without double-counting a subtotal. Different currencies require an explicitly sourced, dated conversion basis approved for the decision; this model supplies no exchange rate or tax conclusion.

At execution, use disjoint exposure categories: already incurred cost, remaining uncancelled contractual liability, and maximum additional spend still executable under open authority but not already counted as liability. Their total must fit the relevant authorised ceiling. Deducting a billed cost twice, or treating a remaining contract value as both a liability and new headroom, is invalid. Include estimated unreported accrual and uncertainty when relevant; absence of a reliable bound prevents a claim that the ceiling is protected. Retained reserve is not executable authority.

S02 distinguishes average daily settings from billing limits and retains a monthly pacing target despite restricted schedules. Exceptions, budget edits and served-versus-billed cost require separate treatment. The bounded numeric illustration is in A11; it is not an account recommendation or verified spend.

A spending-authority reference identifies the real issuer, exact account/campaign or purchase scope, permitted operations, amount/currency/cost basis, validity window, phase, guardrails, allowed reallocation and stop responsibilities. Budget planning, creative selection, repository-write permission and credentials are not substitutes. Production purchases and reservation liabilities need their own applicable authorisation, not just a media daily setting.

Before requesting execution, document how its actual controls respect the authorised bound, including overdelivery, shared budgets, automated changes, reporting latency, stop latency and irrevocable commitments. A pacing alert alone is not a hard cap. When a safe remaining-exposure bound cannot be established for a hard ceiling, block the request and seek a supported execution arrangement; do not invent a platform feature or simply assume that monitoring will prevent overspend.

### Staging and decisions

| Commitment point | Evidence required | Authority and preservation |
|---|---|---|
| Compare plans | Approved brief constraints, cost basis and labelled assumptions | Analysis only; no reservation, purchase or upload implied |
| Commit production or inventory | Exact output/inventory, quote/terms, cancellation liability, rights and readiness dependencies | The relevant owner authorises this commitment; preserve separate media headroom |
| Release a bounded test phase | Complete test/measurement design, usable assets/destination, current restrictions and exact execution controls | Explicit phase spending authority; unreleased phases and reserve stay closed |
| Review or reallocate | Mature, interpretable observations; downstream guardrails; change impact and residual exposure | Only changes inside existing explicit authority may be executed; otherwise obtain a new decision |
| Stop, retire or release further funding | Recorded decision rule, safety findings or justified business decision; remaining liabilities | An authorised executor returns actual action evidence; a recommendation is not a completed stop |

The lean artefacts are an allocation table, authority references and a bounded execution request. No autonomous budget agent, payment system or universal bidding engine is introduced. The actual platform profile and account evidence belong to the external execution layer.

## 7. Alternatives and media/creative coupling

These comparisons are design decisions, not measured campaign comparisons. No live test is required by this stage.

| Alternative pair | Decision and trade-off |
|---|---|
| Channel list versus explicit channel roles | SELECT role-led lines: each expense has an objective and evidence question. A list alone hides duplicated jobs and untested assumptions |
| One broad automated campaign versus tightly bounded placements | CONDITIONAL: automation can serve an authorised optimisation question, but cannot stand in for enforceable eligibility or a clean placement comparison |
| Auction versus reservation/direct inventory | CONDITIONAL: assess flexibility, availability, delivery context, cancellation liability and evidence. Do not equate a reservation promise with business effectiveness |
| Equal test allocation versus unequal/adaptive allocation | CONDITIONAL: choose for the question and record assignment. An optimiser reallocating spend does not validate a causal creative winner |
| Many small cells versus a smaller informative contrast | SELECT sufficient focus when fragmentation makes the question unanswerable; no universal minimum spend or mandatory cell count |
| Maximum reach versus repeated exposure | CONDITIONAL: objective and uncertainty determine the trade-off. Neither maximum reach nor a fixed frequency target is universally optimal |
| Blanket topic blocklist versus reasoned suitability controls | SELECT scoped rules and explicit unknown-context handling. Hard constraints remain hard; preference changes require their owner |
| One asset resized everywhere versus semantic master with contextual adaptations | SELECT the accepted Stage 5 approach: preserve meaning, adapt treatment, verify actual context and avoid unnecessary regeneration |
| Budget field as authority versus separate envelope/commitment/control records | SELECT separation: a planning number cannot protect a ceiling or authorise an account mutation by itself |

Creative and media decisions are coupled, but their authorities do not merge. A search line must consider assembled meaning and intent; a feed line must consider interruption and interface; an audio line must carry essential meaning audibly; a non-clickable video or OOH line needs a usable response mechanism or an explicitly non-transactional objective. These applications inherit Stage 2/5 research, not new universal platform prescriptions. A rejected pairing can retain both a valid concept and a valid placement for different uses.
