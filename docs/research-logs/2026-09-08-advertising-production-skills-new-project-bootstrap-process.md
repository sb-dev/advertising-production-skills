# Advertising Production Skills — New Project Bootstrap Process

**Status:** Bootstrap specification  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 8 September 2026

## 1. Purpose

This process defines how `advertising-production-skills` moves from a project idea to a **specified, scaffolded, benchmarked, installable open-source Production Skills repository** for evidence-driven advertising production.

The project turns an approved business offer and acquisition objective into coherent advertising campaigns rather than treating advertising as isolated copy generation, image prompts or ad-platform button pushing.

```text
approved business / offer / acquisition objective
→ campaign brief
→ audience and context evidence
→ message / claim architecture
→ creative hypotheses
→ cheapest useful ad representations
→ concept selection
→ cross-format creative production
→ media / placement plan
→ destination handoff
→ launch-ready campaign package
→ measurement
→ diagnosis
→ smallest sufficient creative / media correction
→ scaled or retired campaign
```

It owns reusable expertise in campaign briefing, creative strategy, message/claim/proof architecture, ad concepts, variants, format adaptation, media planning, audience hypotheses, campaign experiments, measurement, fatigue management, optimisation and campaign learning.

It does not own the underlying customer/offer/pricing/money model, specialist asset craft, legal conclusions, ad-account administration, autonomous spend, CRM/analytics infrastructure, product truth or Pactwright lifecycle authority.

## 2. Production Skills Family Position

```text
Business Building Skills
→ customer / offer / acquisition objective / economics / growth constraint

Advertising Production Skills
→ campaign strategy / creative system / media plan / tests / optimisation

Narrative / Video / Audio / UIUX / other Production Skills
→ specialist campaign assets and destinations

Legal Skills
→ legal / regulatory constraints

Pactwright
→ optional lifecycle, Contracts and Evidence
```

Business Building owns whether paid advertising is justified, the target customer, offer, price, channel class and economic constraints.

Advertising owns how that approved strategy becomes a campaign: objectives, messages, claims, proof, concepts, placement variants, audience hypotheses, authorised budget staging, tests and campaign learning.

Advertising must not silently change the approved offer, price or target customer to improve ad metrics.

## 3. Governing Principles

### Campaign objective before creative
Define what the campaign must change before producing assets.

### Approved offer before optimisation
Do not compensate for an incoherent offer using increasingly aggressive creative.

### Evidence before claims
Objective claims, testimonials, comparisons, savings, performance statements and scarcity require evidence and appropriate legal/policy review.

### Cheapest useful creative representation
Use message maps, headline variants, rough layouts, scripts, storyboards and mockups before expensive finished assets when uncertainty is still high.

### Creative concept and asset execution are separate
Advertising owns campaign concept and message architecture; specialist Production Skills own final craft where appropriate.

### Learning requires controlled variation
When an experiment aims to identify causality, avoid changing unrelated variables simultaneously unless comparing complete creative systems intentionally.

### Creative fatigue is production state
Track creative lineage, exposure context, refreshes and superseded variants.

### Audience is a hypothesis, not a stereotype
Targeting must be evidence-based, authorised and compatible with platform/legal restrictions.

### Media and creative are coupled
Format, placement, attention context and audience state shape the creative.

### Destination consistency is part of advertising quality
Ad, offer, price, claim, CTA and landing destination must agree.

### Metrics do not outrank business outcomes
Clicks, CTR and platform conversions must be interpreted against qualified response, contribution economics, capacity and downstream value.

### Spend is authorised commitment
The skills may design budgets and staging, but actual spend/account mutation requires authorised execution.

### Preserve approved campaign work
Winning concepts, claims, proof and audience decisions should not be rewritten during unrelated optimisation.

### Correct the smallest responsible layer
Diagnose whether failure belongs to delivery, audience, creative, placement, destination, policy or upstream business strategy.

## 6. Domain Research Stages

### Stage 1 — Boundaries
Resolve campaign briefing, objectives, creative strategy, copy direction, media planning, targeting hypotheses, testing, measurement, optimisation and handoffs with Business Building, Deep Research, Legal, Narrative, Video, Audio, UIUX, Software Engineering and Pactwright.

### Stage 2 — Professional Advertising Practice
Research account planning, creative strategy, copywriting, art direction, brand, direct response, performance marketing, search, social, video/CTV, display/programmatic, OOH, audio, B2B demand generation, app acquisition, retargeting, media planning, ad operations, testing, attribution, brand lift and campaign QA.

### Stage 3 — Campaign Brief / Audience / Objective
Define business objective, advertising objective, approved audience evidence, offer, price, market, budget, economics, schedule, brand requirements, claim/proof inventory, destination and measurement events.

Keep approved business segment, audience hypothesis and platform targeting representation distinct.

### Stage 4 — Message / Claim / Proof / Creative Strategy
Define message hierarchy, claim inventory, proof inventory, objection map, creative territories, hook/angle matrix, CTA architecture and creative hypotheses.

Preserve:
```text
business/customer evidence
→ message hypothesis
→ claim
→ substantiation / proof
→ creative expression
→ placement adaptation
```

### Stage 5 — Creative Production / Cross-Format Adaptation
Define campaign idea → concept → master message/script/visual direction → format variants → placement variants → production assets.

Research static, search, social video, CTV, display, carousel, vertical, native/sponsored and audio formats.

### Stage 6 — Media / Placement / Targeting / Budget
Define channel role, placement role, audience hypothesis, geography, schedule, frequency, reach, optimisation objective, test allocation, exclusions and brand safety.

Business Building owns business-wide channel choice. Advertising owns the campaign media plan. Execution tools own authorised spend/account mutation.

### Stage 7 — Destination and Handoffs
Define continuity requirements for message, price, claim, proof, CTA, tracking and disclosures.

### Stage 8 — Experiment / Measurement / Attribution
Every test defines hypothesis, changed variables, comparison, primary metric, guardrails, downstream business metric, evidence threshold, confounders and decision rule.

Separate platform-reported outcomes from verified downstream outcomes.

### Stage 9 — Optimisation / Fatigue / Campaign Learning
Track parent concept, variants, changed elements, audience/placement context, results, learning and lifecycle state such as active, winner, loser, fatigued, invalid or superseded.

### Stage 10 — Legal / Standards / Platform Policy
Track jurisdiction, advertising standard, platform, product category, claim type, targeting method, policy source, retrieved-at, valid-as-of/effective date and campaign implication.

Legal Skills owns legal conclusions.

## 7. AI / Platform / Tool Research

Research advertising Agent Skills, copy/creative tools, image/video/audio generation, ad libraries, Google/Meta/TikTok/LinkedIn/Reddit/Amazon advertising tooling where relevant, programmatic tooling, creative intelligence, keyword/audience research, media planning, campaign management, analytics, attribution, incrementality and landing-page analysis.

Classify each as `USE`, `ADAPT`, `REFERENCE` or `REJECT`.

## 8. Execution Boundary

Advertising Production Skills own campaign reasoning, briefs, creative hypotheses, claim/proof discipline, format adaptation, media reasoning, experiment design, measurement interpretation, diagnosis and learning preservation.

External systems own account administration, asset rendering, campaign upload, bidding/spend, ad serving, data collection, conversion tracking, attribution computation and hosting.

Do not build an ad server, DSP, CRM, analytics platform or universal ad-platform API prematurely.


Every mature pack requires a showcase, exact prompt, behavioural/negative evals and core-vs-pack comparison.

## 11. Progressive Examples

Target 5 levels × 3 primary examples = 15.

```text
L1 bounded advertising unit
L2 coherent creative test
L3 complete campaign
L4 diagnose and repair campaign performance
L5 full advertising-production thesis
```

Candidate L5 cases:

```text
Kakeibo consumer subscription launch campaign
one-person FDE consultancy B2B campaign
Production Skills open-source ecosystem adoption/commercial campaign
```

The 15 examples should cover B2B/B2C, brand/performance, search/social/video/audio/display, creative strategy, claims/proof, adaptation, media planning, audiences, testing, measurement, fatigue, optimisation, destination handoff, legal/policy constraints, cross-domain asset production and repair.

Every primary example requires the complete copyable prompt.

## 12. Canonical Stress Tests

### Kakeibo
Test consumer trust, subscription/refund consistency, financial sensitivity, acquisition, claims, privacy/targeting and retention-aware downstream quality.

### One-person FDE consultancy
Test high-ticket B2B, narrow ICP, qualification, credibility/proof, capacity constraints and longer sales cycles.

### Production Skills ecosystem
Test open-source adoption, developer channels, education/services/marketplace messaging and brand/performance tension.

## 13. Evaluation Architecture

Separate:

```text
deterministic campaign validation
creative quality
campaign reasoning
business alignment
claim / legal / policy validity
experiment quality
measurement quality
optimisation / fatigue
preservation / repair
Extension Pack behaviour
end-to-end campaign production
installation integrity
```

Fail persuasive-looking work when it relies on unsupported claims, false scarcity, fake testimonials, misleading prices, invalid targeting, stale material policy assumptions, bad experiment design, vanity metrics or downstream business harm.

Do not publish one universal advertising quality score.

## 19. Non-Goals

`advertising-production-skills` is not:

- a replacement for Business Building Skills;
- a generic marketing department;
- a CRM;
- an email-marketing platform;
- a DSP or ad server;
- an autonomous media-spend agent;
- an attribution vendor;
- a customer-data platform;
- a universal audience graph;
- a universal copy generator;
- a legal advertising-compliance authority;
- a one-number campaign score;
- a platform-specific automation repository.

## 20. Success Criterion

The resulting project should make advertising production:

```text
more strategy-led
more traceable to approved business decisions
more evidence-backed in its claims
cheaper to validate before expensive asset production
more coherent across formats and placements
more disciplined about experiments
more aware of platform / legal constraints
more resistant to vanity metrics
more capable of preserving campaign learning
more precise about cross-domain handoffs
more efficient to diagnose and repair
```

while remaining a lean Production Skills project rather than a universal advertising platform.
