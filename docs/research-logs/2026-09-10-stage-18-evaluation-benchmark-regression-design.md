## 4. Twelve independent evaluation contracts

Every finding identifies criterion, subject/revision, actual inspected evidence, method, result, limitation and smallest responsible repair. `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED` and justified `NOT APPLICABLE` apply to criteria. Execution state is separate. A complete evaluator may correctly reject a defective ad; the ad itself does not pass as a result.

### D01. Deterministic campaign validation

Inputs are the exact task, declared fixture/profile and actual output tree. Check required fields and counts, source/reference resolution, IDs/revisions, units, format properties, constrained text lengths, calculation invariants and authorised file changes. Required-but-unknown values must be explicit knowledge states with consequences, not empty placeholders or zero. For a complete brief account for all thirteen areas; for a complete test all nine fields; for a policy context all ten fields. The source task determines which full model is required for a bounded operation.

Pass only each property actually checked. Missing requested files or unresolved required links fail or block the appropriate requirement. A schema-correct claim is not substantiated by that success. Repair the exact field, relationship or export through its owner, preserving unaffected content. Fixture text character limits are exercise rules, not a current platform-wide policy.

### D02. Creative quality

Inputs are actual copy or the requested media at its stated fidelity, objective, brand direction, claim map and placement context. Inspect attention order, proposition comprehension, distinct concept mechanism, fit to the audience hypothesis, appropriate brand association, usable action, qualification prominence and craft. For motion inspect the complete temporal sequence, relevant transitions and reduced viewing context; for audio actually listen to the file and assess intelligibility, timing, pronunciation and masked qualifications. A metadata probe or transcript cannot replace listening.

Record evidence-linked strengths, defects and trade-offs per relevant property. A plausible creative preference is not consumer response, brand lift or a market winner. Pass the declared draft or final craft criteria only at the fidelity inspected. Missing modality access blocks its inspection. Repair the responsible words, layout, beat, caption, pronunciation or mix; do not regenerate all siblings for one local defect.

### D03. Campaign reasoning

Inputs are approved business/brief facts, audience evidence, alternatives and the produced decision rationale. Check the evidence-to-message-to-claim-to-expression chain, why each concept could address the question, meaningful alternatives, media/creative coupling, uncertainty and the cheapest useful next representation. Three cosmetic headline changes cannot satisfy a request for three distinct concepts.

Inspect the returned explanation and observable decisions, not private hidden reasoning. Accept different sound approaches when they preserve constraints and address the objective. Unsupported audience stereotypes, ignored contradictory evidence, circular proof or a plan shaped only by available APIs fail the affected inference. Repair the premise, comparison or handoff, not the approved commercial strategy without authority.

### D04. Business alignment

Compare the produced campaign with exact approved offer, price, commercial segment, channel class, economic basis, capacity and outcome horizon. Recompute disjoint budget allocations and exposure categories when present. Check qualified, retained and fulfilled outcomes rather than rewarding clicks or attributed revenue alone. Keep missing costs, refunds, prices and follow-up unknown; a signed or booked item is not automatically recognised revenue or profit.

Any hidden commercial change or commitment beyond available authority fails. Negative contribution or constrained capacity cannot be averaged away by creative quality. Advertising proposes the bounded correction or returns the upstream decision; it does not invent a discount, overtime, subcontractor or lifetime value.

### D05. Claim, legal and policy validity

Inspect exact explicit and implied assertions, proof scope, qualifications, rights/identity and the current or frozen policy context appropriate to the test. Trace each material assertion to support for that population, product, market and period. Separate a specification, implementation receipt, real testimonial and synthetic example. Check the ten-field context and owner decision when the task depends on it; a retrieved policy title or platform acceptance is not Legal clearance.

Unsupported claims, false scarcity, invented endorsement, misleading price/renewal, hidden material information, prohibited targeting or stale material assumptions fail. An unresolved legal question remains blocked for that scope and is returned with exact facts to Legal. Deterministic presence checks do not adjudicate law. Repair the offending assertion or evidence dependency and recheck its expressions, not all valid campaign facts.

### D06. Experiment quality

Inspect all nine test fields: hypothesis, changed variables, comparison, primary metric, guardrails, downstream metric, evidence threshold, confounders and decision rule. Additionally inspect assignment/analysis unit, eligible population, timing and maturity, feasible observation, interference, changed-variable control and the declared uncertainty framework. A combined treatment can be valid for a combined question, not falsely called a hook-only contrast.

Incomplete design, unsupported sample adequacy, post-hoc winner rules or attribution promoted to incrementality fail the relevant conclusion. Unknown feasibility can correctly block a proposed live run while leaving a well-specified design complete. Repair the comparison or observation requirement; never fabricate power, respondents or spend authority. The selected evidence threshold belongs to the fixture/campaign, not a global p-value default.

### D07. Measurement quality

Compare source event definitions, population, environment, units, currency, windows, attribution, duplicate policy, maturity and downstream qualification before totals. Preserve raw observations, corrections and unexplained residuals. Check missing, privacy-limited, modelled, immature and observed-zero states independently. Ratios across visitors, attempts and installations are labelled operational ratios, not person conversion without a valid identity mapping.

Use exact arithmetic for stipulated examples; document any permitted rounding separately. A zero denominator gives an undefined ratio, not zero. Missing numerator/denominator yields unavailable evidence. Recompute an explicitly requested uncertainty calculation from the stated method; do not present point estimates as intervals. Repair collection/definition or interpretation through its owner. Data minimisation and restricted export remain hard constraints even when more detailed data would simplify analysis.

### D08. Optimisation and fatigue

Inputs include source-separated results, contextual exposure, revisions, change history, accepted objectives and alternative explanations. Inspect measurement health before diagnosing delivery, audience, creative, placement, destination, policy or upstream business. Check whether apparent deterioration follows a changed mix, invalid event, immature cohort, real exposure pattern or other supported cause.

Keep delivery, performance assessment, evidence validity, fatigue finding and succession separate. Age, frequency or a low dashboard rating alone does not prove fatigue. A paused recommendation is not actual containment. Pass a limited operational diagnosis only within its evidence; block unsupported causal mechanisms. Repair or investigate the smallest responsible layer and retain historical valid evidence and withdrawn conclusions.

### D09. Preservation and repair

Inputs are immutable before-state, declared changed/fixed fields, exact defect, owner authority and actual after-state. Compare required fixed bytes where the task explicitly fixes a string or file. For semantic preservation inspect claim meaning, terms, source dependencies and unaffected siblings even when serialization changes. For E05 compare decoded shared-body frames/timing, not container hashes that may differ after encoding.

Pass only when the actual correction resolves the defect, reopens affected approvals and leaves unrelated accepted work intact. A repair proposal is not applied external state. Excessive regeneration, silent source mutation, lost history or a new commercial proposition disguised as a local fix fails. Restore the unaffected baseline and rerun only the affected plus dependent checks.

### D10. Extension Pack behaviour

Bind exact installed core/pack revisions and actual selected resources. Inspect requested activation, non-activation, compatible local loading, original precedence and the three named observables for each pack. Verify build, optimise and evaluate use the compatible grammar within their own responsibility, rather than claiming all skills were exercised by one build output.

Run all six conditions for each selected pack, using matched facts, requested fidelity and resource limits. Judge actual task-relevant differences, not metadata labels, length or slang. Report useful specialisation, no material difference, regression or inconclusive with evidence. Equal competent outputs are valid observations but not demonstrated incremental benefit or grounds to claim pack maturity. Any hard invariant regression fails regardless of specialisation.

### D11. End-to-end campaign production

Inspect the complete requested chain: accepted fixture/business inputs, brief, message/claim/proof, actual cheap concepts, selection/preference with its real authority, representative actual variants, media/audience test, measurement contract, evaluation and bounded correction. An offline launch package binds revisions and unresolved real actions; it does not imply serving.

All required actual files and modality inspections must exist. Missing WAV/PNG/MP4 cannot pass through an otherwise excellent plan. A source-bound stress audit may correctly block real activation without refusing its requested local design. Pass only the requested production scope, with separate business, craft, measurement and authority findings. Local source evidence, a manifest, checksum and provider success each prove different properties.

### D12. Installation integrity

Test each of four skills independently in a clean consumer directory, first from local source and then from the pinned GitHub revision. Record actual installer/host versions, exact command, discovered skill, installed paths and a bounded invocation that reads its own required resources. Remove access to the source checkout and sibling skills during the resource test. Check every offered pack's identity/resources and core-only non-activation.

Verify update/uninstall preserves unrelated consumer files and introduces no secrets, account configuration or unrequested runtime. A directory copy is not host discovery; installer exit zero is not successful domain use. Do not claim support for an untested host or remote route. Repair packaging/installation at its owning layer and rerun all affected selective installs. Stage 25 must perform these tests; this stage does not assert they ran.

## 7. Grader validity and reliability

Before using a grader for acceptance, check a known valid output, a semantically valid alternative and a seeded defect for its actual property. Examples: changed price must fail; harmless wording changes must not fail exactness unless the task fixes those bytes; an absent required WAV must fail production; an actually supplied but semantically wrong qualification must fail even if format passes. Verify that the same test detects its negative mutation, and that no criterion can be satisfied by writing PASS into a report.

Use deterministic code for exact math, schema, presence and byte invariants. Use structured semantic review for meaning and decision fit, with actual evidence spans, frames or time ranges. Use real specialist/human decisions where the task requires that authority. A model can identify issues and uncertainty; it cannot impersonate an approver or certify legal compliance. Identify whether a review is self-review, another model, actual human review or a technical tool result.

For pairwise model judging, anonymise condition labels where feasible, inspect both presentation orders and investigate inconsistent findings. Require criterion-specific evidence rather than rewarding length or similarity to the judge's preferred prose. Keep disagreement unresolved or obtain the proper reviewer; do not automatically average it away. No claim of human-calibrated grading is allowed without actual calibration records. These controls are prompted by R02's limited abstract findings and are project design, not a reproduced statistical remedy.

Minimum coverage means every mandatory case runs, not that one pass establishes reliability. For a stronger reliability or comparative claim, predeclare repeated trials, sampling, resource budget and analysis appropriate to that claim. Report case denominators, all attempts, blocked cases, first-attempt and repaired outcomes separately. Do not silently drop difficult cases or multiply per-trial estimates under unverified independence. A matched single comparison supports only its observed outputs; it does not prove population-level pack superiority or real campaign uplift.

## 8. Regression and change-impact rules

Freeze accepted fixtures and source identities. When a source, skill, pack or output contract changes, create a new revision and run the affected dependency closure. Keep a frozen historical-source suite separate from a current-source readiness audit; a correct historical answer is not automatically current. Correct a demonstrably erroneous oracle through an attributable owning decision, preserving its prior history and rerunning affected tests. Never relax a requirement because the model failed it.

Use exact golden strings only for explicitly fixed copy/terms/IDs. Use semantic criteria for legitimate alternative wording and composition. Check invariants such as fixed subject, units, claims, output fidelity and authority across every repair. Do not require pixel-identical novel artwork except for an explicitly fixed region/sequence such as E05's shared body. Tool or host replacement must rerun its relevant capability/security and installation checks; it is not a harmless metadata edit.

## 9. Acceptance gates and prohibited false success

The first core vertical at Stage 23 must deliver its entire original chain, including 2–3 actual cheap concepts, actual variants, test/measurement contract, independent evaluation and bounded correction. Its subset does not complete Stage 24. Stage 24 must execute all fifteen primary examples and all eight selected pack showcases/conditions, with requested files and inspections, plus the canonical and necessary regression/authoring checks for the claimed coverage. A stage or release acceptance requires every mandatory applicable criterion for its actual scope to pass. A blocked mandatory output prevents that acceptance. A correctly blocked external action inside an explicitly local-only task is expected safe behaviour and does not itself make the local task fail. Record both task fulfilment and subject readiness. No rule permits a PNG to substitute for a video, a script for audio, an installer for a working skill, or a report for a real result.

Unsupported claims, false scarcity, fake testimonials, misleading prices, invalid targeting, stale material policy assumptions, bad experiment design, vanity metrics and downstream business harm each have explicit coverage in the catalogue and D04–D08. Persuasive presentation never offsets one of these defects.

No new ad server, DSP, CRM, attribution engine, account store, general benchmark platform or Pactwright lifecycle machinery is introduced. Ordinary execution remains possible in a compatible existing host. The repository can publish accurate evidence of what was actually demonstrated, but cannot promote registry maturity, release or PR readiness before the original final audit and required owner authorization.
