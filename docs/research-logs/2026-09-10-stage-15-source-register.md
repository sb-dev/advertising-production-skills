## Discovery and access notes

Research compared response/brand, role/qualification, app use, launch phases, prior-interaction states, local fulfilment and creator expression. Broad search returned practitioner lists and community answers, but they were not used as technical or policy authority. A Google URL initially opened as “About explanations”, rather than the intended targeting page; it is not cited for targeting behaviour. The accepted Stage 3/6 representation rules remain the relevant enforcement contract.

Two follow-up web requests briefly failed with service-unavailable errors. A later successful read obtained the relevant Google segment and FTC announcement bodies; no failed call is recorded as a successful access. R07’s direct verification redirect was not bypassed and its evidence remains explicitly search-returned. The IPA and TikTok linked PDFs and underlying empirical studies were not opened; no unseen charts or methodology are claimed.

The synthesis does not adopt exact allocation, audience, frequency, sample-size, conversion-delay or success numbers from provider or practitioner material. Fixture values are clearly stipulated test inputs, not research findings. Campaign-specific law, consent, platform availability, actual performance, cost and rights require the owning evidence at execution; this design supplies none of them.
