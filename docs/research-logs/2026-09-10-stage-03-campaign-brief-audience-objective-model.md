## 3. Representation and minimum structure

Use a readable Markdown brief with stable section/record identifiers and links to exact source revisions. Tables or structured blocks may encode repeated records, but the semantics below do not require a particular parser, database, universal schema, graph runtime or ad-platform API. A small brief can be one file; a large consuming project can link its existing business, evidence and approval records instead of copying them.

The brief contains a header, the 13 areas in section 5, the three audience records in section 6, a scoped acceptance decision and unresolved issues. An upstream record can satisfy a content requirement by reference only when the exact revision and relevant content are accessible to the authorised reviewer. A bare link to a changing homepage or an inaccessible private record cannot establish verification. Do not copy restricted source material into this public repository to solve an access problem.

| Header item | Meaning and required content |
|---|---|
| Brief identity | Stable campaign/brief identifier, consuming-project location, revision, author/responsible owner and creation/update time with timezone |
| Work context | `real` or `synthetic`; the latter must be unambiguous in the header and all exported evidence/approvals |
| Requested decision | What this brief is asking to proceed with: bounded analysis, concept development, a production commitment or another identified step |
| Business baseline | Exact versions of the commercial objective, approved segment, offer, price, channel class and economic constraints being consumed |
| Scope | Intended market, objective, audiences, time window and outputs, plus exclusions and work explicitly outside the brief |
| Dependency references | Relevant source/evidence, decision, destination and measurement records; no credentials or raw customer lists |
| Acceptance and issues | Decision scope, approver/authority reference, affected revision, unresolved issues, affected actions and assigned resolution owners |

Each required area is either populated, explicitly unknown/conflicted, or explicitly not applicable with a reason. Empty strings, missing keys, zero, “TBC” and not-applicable are not interchangeable. In particular, zero price or budget is a real value only when supplied as such; it must not conceal missing information.

## 4. Provenance, knowledge and approval

Material facts, assumptions, proposals and constraints need identifiable provenance. Reuse one source reference across related values when its scope is explicit; do not create a separate bureaucratic object for every word. Values that can change commercial meaning, eligibility, claims, commitment or evaluation require revision-level traceability.

| Dimension | Required distinction |
|---|---|
| Source | Source identifier/location, version or captured-at timestamp, relevant passage/data definition, owner, accessible evidence and use restrictions |
| Knowledge basis | `observed`, `supplied`, `derived`, `hypothesis`, `unknown`, `conflicted` or `not-applicable`; an observation can still have uncertainty or bias |
| Scope and validity | Which population/product/market/period the value covers; effective/expiry dates where present; review trigger and known limitations |
| Use decision | `pending`, `approved-for-scope`, `rejected`, `expired` or `superseded`, with the actual authority/evidence reference where an approval is asserted |
| Derivation | Inputs, units, calculation or reasoning and uncertainty; a derived projection stays a projection even when its use is approved |
| Dependency | Which brief field, hypothesis, claim, proposed action or evaluation relies on the value |

Approval does not strengthen evidence. A business owner can accept a weak research input for a bounded exploratory question without making its conclusion a fact. Conversely, strong evidence does not confer rights to use a person's testimonial, permission to target an audience or authority to spend. Keep evidential support and permitted use separately inspectable. [Stage1]

An approval record identifies its issuer and authority, decision time, exact subject/revision, purpose, scope, conditions, validity period and decision evidence. Where the consuming project already records these, link them. A skill's positive evaluation is not substituted for an owner decision. A changed subject or breached condition requires re-review of the affected scope; old approval is preserved as historical evidence, not silently attached to the new value.

Unknown inputs carry a question, resolution owner, evidence needed, affected action and review point. Conflicting inputs retain both source references and the conflict; latest retrieval time or a more convenient answer does not decide authority. Not-applicable needs a semantic reason and the responsible acceptance decision. For example, a no-charge educational campaign can have an explicitly approved zero-price offer; that does not make its production costs or business objective disappear.

Synthetic approvals identify a fictitious fixture authority and are valid only inside a modelling exercise. They never grant live authority. No customer research, legal clearance, production acceptance, account mutation or spending approval is supplied by this document.

## 5. The 13 brief areas

### C01. Business objective

**Meaning and minimum content.** The commercial or organisational outcome supplied by Business Building/project ownership: outcome statement, relevant customer/market, baseline and target where known, unit, time horizon, business success measure, capacity and other guardrails, priority and source/approval. “Increase revenue” without population, period or a way to judge it is a question to resolve, not a complete objective. A target is an intention, never a result.

**Ownership and links.** Advertising consumes the approved objective and links each advertising objective to it. A numeric target must have an owner and basis; where no defensible numeric target exists, the owner may approve a specific decision/learning objective and its evidence requirement rather than invent a number. Non-commercial adoption or education is permitted when that is the actual approved objective.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** No approved objective means campaign strategy cannot be accepted as aligned. A conflict between growth, margin and capacity goes to the business owner. Advertising can expose the trade-off but cannot choose a different objective simply because a platform can optimise it. Detailed test thresholds are Stage 8 work, not assumed by this field.

### C02. Advertising objective

**Meaning and minimum content.** The change advertising is intended to cause in awareness, understanding, consideration or a specified response, for a named audience/context and horizon. Record the business-objective reference, proposed causal mechanism, observable indicator, intended direction/target where supported, primary advertising outcome, guardrails and downstream outcome relationship. State whether the indicator is a direct observation, proxy or planned study requirement.

**Ownership and links.** Advertising proposes and evaluates this translation within the business baseline; the responsible project/brief owner accepts its scope. A platform campaign objective or bidding event is a later execution mapping, not the definition of the advertising objective. A brief may support multiple objectives, but must name priority and conflict rules rather than label every metric primary.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** An unsupported business link, contradictory priorities or a proxy that rewards the wrong response prevents objective acceptance. A brand objective need not promise immediate sales, but must explain its business relationship and an accountable evidence horizon. A metric target does not supply an approved test design, measurement implementation or forecast. [Stage2]

### C03. Approved audience evidence

**Meaning and minimum content.** Identify each audience-relevant input by source/revision, method or collection context, observed population, market/time, finding, limitations, contradictions and permission for the intended use. Record which assertion it supports and what remains inference. Where a sample or method is unknown, say so; do not imply statistical representativeness. Evidence may concern needs, language, contexts, objections or past behaviour without identifying individuals.

**Ownership and links.** Research/source owners supply provenance; the authorised owner approves its use in the brief. Advertising assesses relevance to the proposed hypothesis. Evidence-use approval is not confirmation of truth, consent for data activation or a declaration that an audience stereotype is accurate. References point to appropriately controlled source records, not copied personal data.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** Unsupported audience truth cannot be stated as observed. Record a bounded hypothesis and the evidence needed to examine it, only within already approved commercial and data-use boundaries. Stale, incompatible or materially conflicting evidence blocks the inference or action depending on it; unaffected facts remain available. Section 6 defines how evidence links the three separate audience layers.

### C04. Offer

**Meaning and minimum content.** Exact approved exchange: product/service or other deliverable, relevant version, beneficiary/eligibility, included and excluded scope, fulfilment conditions, availability/capacity, material commitments, cancellation/refund/guarantee terms where applicable, and source/approval. Preserve the actual offer name and substantive terms rather than replacing them with a marketing proposition.

**Ownership and links.** Business Building/project ownership controls the offer and product truth. Advertising can decide which supported value to emphasise, but emphasis cannot change the exchange. Link the price record, relevant market, inventory of supported claims, destination and validity window. A free resource still needs its actual access conditions and any subsequent paid proposition distinguished.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** Unknown inclusions, unsupported functionality or contradictory refund terms block dependent copy/production decisions. Ask the owner for the exact input rather than inventing a favourable assumption. Commercial change needs a new upstream decision and an impact review; this brief must not turn an unapproved feature roadmap into a current benefit. Stage 4 will build message/proof architecture on this baseline.

### C05. Price

**Meaning and minimum content.** Amount or approved price basis, currency, billable unit, charging/billing period, fees and tax presentation supplied by the owner, upfront versus recurring charges, any minimum commitment, trial/introductory and renewal terms, eligibility, validity window, and relation to refund/cancellation terms. Where pricing is variable or quoted individually, record that approved model and the evidence/conditions for any advertised starting price; do not manufacture a fixed figure.

**Ownership and links.** The business owner supplies and approves commercial terms. Advertising preserves their meaning across claims and destination requirements. The model captures tax/fee treatment as supplied; it does not calculate legal obligations or assert that a particular presentation is legally sufficient. Applicable legal assessment remains separate.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** An unknown price is not zero. A price change invalidates the dependent commercial snapshot and expressions, not unrelated audience evidence. Contradictions between the ad's proposed price and the destination require resolution before that execution path proceeds. Material terms must remain available to subsequent copy, placement and Legal review; “the landing page explains it” is not a model-level waiver. [Stage1]

### C06. Market

**Meaning and minimum content.** Intended commercial geography, language/locale, distribution or service availability, relevant product/platform/store environment, eligible customer context, exclusions and the period for which those facts hold. Distinguish the market in which the offer can actually be fulfilled from a media tool's geographic selection. Separate a proposed expansion from an already approved served market.

**Ownership and links.** Business ownership supplies market/service boundaries; Advertising references them when defining its audience and campaign. Link price/currency, offer availability, language/brand requirements and destination routing. Record where jurisdiction/category questions require Legal input rather than making the brief itself a legal opinion.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** Unknown fulfilment availability, unsupported locale or contradictory geographic constraints block the affected plan. A translation is not approval to sell in a new market. When a platform representation cannot enforce the required area or eligibility, record the mapping gap and reject that execution route until resolved; do not expand the commercial market to fit convenient tooling.

### C07. Budget

**Meaning and minimum content.** Planning envelope with currency, period, included cost categories, total ceiling, proposed allocation/reserve, committed amounts supplied by the owner, and separate approval references. Distinguish media, production, measurement and other included costs; explicitly identify any budget funded outside this envelope so that “remaining media budget” does not hide production liabilities. Use exact monetary values or clearly labelled estimates, not implied exchange rates.

**Ownership and links.** The business/budget owner supplies constraints. Advertising can propose campaign staging within them. A budget record states whether money is merely considered, approved for planning or authorised for a specific commitment. A planning ceiling and an executable platform daily setting are not synonymous; the execution owner must verify how its controls honour the authorised scope.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** Currency, period or inclusion ambiguity blocks allocation/commitment reasoning that depends on it. Proposed included allocations plus reserve cannot exceed the declared envelope without a new owner decision. Estimates must not be reported as actual spend. No account mutation, spending, production purchase or paid tool use is authorised by this field alone; detailed media staging belongs to Stage 6.

### C08. Economics

**Meaning and minimum content.** Approved business constraints used to interpret acquisition: relevant outcome/unit, contribution basis, known acquisition or payback limits, capacity/fulfilment restrictions, period/cohort, fixed versus variable costs where relevant, assumptions and uncertainty. Distinguish revenue from contribution, a first transaction from projected lifetime value, and booked response from collected/retained value. Include only metrics useful to the actual objective; no universal money model is required.

**Ownership and links.** Business Building/project ownership supplies economic definitions and authorises assumptions used for a decision. Advertising links the configured or proposed response measure to those definitions rather than recomputing a business strategy. A derived allowable cost needs transparent inputs/units and an owner-approved basis; missing future retention must not be filled with a confident lifetime-value estimate.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** Unknown economics can be an explicit learning limit, but cannot support a claim of profitable scaling or an unauthorised commitment. A zero-charge adoption campaign may instead have an approved cost/effort guardrail and capacity constraint. Conflicting economics or downstream harm go upstream; a lower cost per proxy event does not override them. [Stage1] [Stage2]

### C09. Schedule

**Meaning and minimum content.** Intended campaign/offer window, timezone, important production/review/launch deadlines, availability or seasonal constraints supplied by the owner, observation horizon and dependency dates. Distinguish a requested launch date, confirmed readiness and actual launch. A date without timezone or a planned window without a clear boundary convention is insufficient where that ambiguity affects delivery or price validity.

**Ownership and links.** Advertising proposes a feasible sequence inside business availability and explicit external commitments. The relevant production, destination, approval and execution owners confirm their dependencies. Measurement may legitimately continue after media stops; the analysis window and the offer validity window are separate.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** A deadline does not waive evidence or approval. An offer expiring before the planned creative stops must be reconciled by the relevant owner; do not silently extend it. Unknown instrument readiness or impossible production dependencies block the affected launch commitment but need not erase earlier valid research. Stage 5/6/8 details refine production, serving and observation schedules without changing upstream dates invisibly.

### C10. Brand requirements

**Meaning and minimum content.** Applicable identity/voice principles, recognisable elements to preserve, mandatory or prohibited expressions, naming conventions, accessibility requirements supplied by the project, approved references, usage rights/constraints and relevant brand-owner decisions. Separate a binding rule from an inspirational reference, a selected creative direction from a supplied asset, and a present capability from an aspirational positioning statement.

**Ownership and links.** The brand/project owner controls changes to these inputs. Advertising records their implications for the objective and concept brief; specialists execute within them. Reference the exact asset/guide version and scope when relied upon. The brief need not copy complete brand manuals, font files, licensed assets or personal likenesses into this repository.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** Missing brand guidance must not be invented and labelled approved. Record the uncertainty and ask for a decision only when a real campaign action requires it. An apparent conflict between brand preference and substantiation or legal restriction is escalated; the preference cannot override the restriction. Local layout repairs preserve unaffected brand decisions. Detailed creative selection and production acceptance remain later-stage responsibilities.

### C11. Claim/proof inventory

**Meaning and minimum content.** At brief entry, enumerate every proposed factual advertising assertion relied upon, its exact meaning/scope, product/offer version, linked evidence references, qualifications, known limitations, owner and review/use status. The inventory includes pricing, comparative, performance, testimonial, guarantee or availability statements when present; it must also record known unsupported or prohibited assertions so they are not regenerated as attractive copy.

**Ownership and links.** Product/business owners supply truth and supporting material; Advertising records the claim-to-evidence relationship; Legal or other appropriate authorities review within their remit. Evidence existence, evidential adequacy, claim approval, generated expression and publication approval are different facts. An empty inventory needs an explicit explanation and review of whether the proposal in fact implies claims.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** Missing proof blocks the dependent assertion's execution path. It does not licence fabricated statistics or a “creative” testimonial. Approved evidence for one version/population does not prove a stronger claim or another offer. Stage 4 owns the detailed claim/proof architecture, objections, messages and hypotheses; Stage 10 owns applicable legal/policy context. This field is the complete intake contract, not a replacement for either.

### C12. Destination

**Meaning and minimum content.** Intended endpoint or experience, stable identifier/version or explicit proposed location, responsible owner, market/locale, intended action, offer/price/claim/proof/disclosure requirements and relevant routing/tracking dependencies. State current existence/readiness: a planned page, design, deployed endpoint and verified journey are not interchangeable. An offline call or visit can be a destination if it is the actual response route.

**Ownership and links.** Advertising specifies promise continuity; UIUX/Software Engineering or the appropriate operator owns design, implementation and technical evidence. Link commercial and claim records instead of maintaining competing price/proof sources. Record the expected user-visible promise and event relationship so that “same URL” cannot falsely imply “same destination meaning”.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** Unknown destination behaviour blocks launch/journey claims that depend on it. Bounded concept work can reference an explicit destination requirement without pretending the destination exists. A changed price, broken route or contradictory qualification is routed to the responsible owner and affected checks reopened. Stage 7 defines detailed handoff and continuity evaluation; this intake makes its required inputs explicit.

### C13. Measurement events

**Meaning and minimum content.** For each event/observation required by the objective, define its identifier, semantic meaning, entity/unit counted, qualifying and disqualifying conditions, observation source/owner, time/window, relationship to the business outcome and status of implementation/verification. Name distinct observation layers: platform-reported delivery/response, first-party business observation, survey or other study outcome, or a derived measure. Record aggregation, duplicate handling and value/currency meaning where relevant, without collecting personal data here.

**Ownership and links.** Advertising defines the information needed and assesses relevance; Software Engineering/analytics or the relevant study owner supplies implementation and evidence. A form submission is not automatically a qualified lead, an install is not retained usage, and a paid event is not necessarily non-refunded contribution. A platform event mapping must identify the exact meaning/goal it will drive and any gap to the business measure.

**Missing/conflicting behaviour.** A named event with no verified implementation is a requirement, not available data. Unknown definitions, mismatched windows, duplicate counts or unverifiable downstream outcomes restrict conclusions and can block launch/measurement commitments. Record the verification evidence required and owner. Stage 8 defines complete experiment/attribution contracts; this field neither computes attribution nor invents campaign results. [Stage2]

## 6. Three audience layers, not one persona

### A01. Approved business segment

This is the upstream definition of who the business intends and is able to serve, including eligibility, exclusions, relevant needs/context and the exact authority approving that scope. Minimum content: segment ID/revision, inclusion and exclusion rules, served market, link to the offer/economics, evidence references, owner and scoped approval. Separate hard eligibility boundaries from business preferences that remain hypotheses; an attractive descriptive label is not an operational rule.

Advertising cannot redefine the segment. A proposal to reach a new customer class, promise a different service or weaken an exclusion returns to Business Building/project ownership. Advertising audiences can include authorised influencers or buyers acting for users when the approved business scope explicitly permits those roles. Do not equate “audience must remain within scope” with an unsupported assumption that every recipient must personally be the end user; unapproved role expansion still needs an upstream decision.

### A02. Advertising audience hypothesis

This is a testable proposition about which authorised people/roles, situations or interests may respond to a particular communication. Minimum content: hypothesis ID/revision, parent business-segment reference, relevant context/need or proposed response, supporting/contradicting evidence, inference strength, expected observation, exclusions, owner and decision status. It must explain why the hypothesised response advances the campaign objective, not merely describe a demographic persona.

A hypothesis is permitted to be uncertain. Its uncertainty must be explicit and the next evidence-generating step bounded; it cannot be relabelled as discovered customer truth because an AI model produces a plausible description. Business approval of the segment does not automatically approve every targeting method or inference. Avoid unnecessary personal or sensitive attributes; a contextual hypothesis often does not need individual-level data at all.

### A03. Platform targeting representation

This is the proposed or verified mapping from the audience hypothesis and hard constraints into a particular executor's capabilities. Minimum content: representation ID/revision, parent hypothesis and segment, platform/product/profile if selected, mapping of each inclusion/exclusion/context rule, enforcement classification, known expansion/automation behaviour, data-source/use authority references where applicable, account/configuration evidence and current verification status.

For each required rule, record `enforced`, `signal-only`, `unsupported` or `unverified`, with evidence. These labels are model-defined requirements, not claims about any particular provider. Unknown platform selection is valid as a planning unknown; it cannot masquerade as an executable audience. Only documented, relevant capability plus the required configuration/verification evidence supports an enforcement claim. A generic product description does not establish an actual account's controls.

A hard restriction mapped only to a signal, unsupported setting or unverified control prevents execution relying on that restriction. Advertising may propose a different authorised execution route or return an irreconcilable constraint to its owner. It cannot treat a weaker representation as equivalent, silently drop exclusions or use data merely because upload is technically possible. No platform or data provider is selected in this stage.

### Relationship and change rules

| Relationship | Required check | Failed check and owner |
|---|---|---|
| Segment → hypothesis | The hypothesised audience/role and expected response remain within the approved commercial scope | New segment/offer or role boundary goes to Business Building/project owner |
| Evidence → hypothesis | The evidence scope and limitations support the stated inference, or uncertainty is honestly retained | Unsupported inference returns to Advertising/Research; unavailable evidence is not manufactured |
| Hypothesis → representation | The mapping preserves the intended hypothesis and explicitly classifies hard constraints versus hints | Mapping/implementation gap goes to Advertising and the authorised executor |
| Data-use authority → representation | Any requested data activation is authorised for this purpose and scope | Missing authority blocks the affected action; Legal/data owner resolves it |
| Representation → delivery evidence | The actual setup and observed context correspond to the version evaluated | Execution mismatch is repaired by its owner; resulting evidence retains its limitation |

Every downstream reference retains the parent IDs/revisions. Renaming a segment, copying its description into a platform field or observing high response does not merge these objects. Revision changes require an impact check, not automatic acceptance or indiscriminate rewriting of unaffected work. Later media and legal models add detail to these relationships without removing their separate identities.

## 7. Objective and measurement alignment

The brief makes this chain inspectable:

```text
approved business outcome and guardrails
→ intended advertising-induced change
→ audience hypothesis within approved scope
→ observable event or study requirement
→ proposed execution goal/mapping
→ platform observations and verified downstream observations kept distinct
→ bounded interpretation against the original objective
```

Each arrow needs a reason or dependency, not just a repeated metric label. A campaign might seek a qualified conversation that later contributes to revenue; record that qualification and lag rather than call a downloaded document a sale. An awareness objective might require a study; record feasibility and uncertainty rather than promise a survey outcome that has not been measured. These are modelling distinctions, not claims that an example campaign will work. [Stage2]

For each primary advertising objective, identify its business parent, measure/observation, unit, population, horizon, ownership and guardrail. Where a numeric target is unknown, capture the proposed decision criterion and its approval state. A target, historical baseline, forecast and actual observation must use distinct labels and provenance. A forecast requires inputs, assumptions and uncertainty; this stage supplies none for a real project.

The event-to-objective mapping records whether an event is an outcome, proxy, diagnostic or guardrail. An execution system's optimisation goal is separately mapped to those semantics. Changing the goal or event definition requires a revision and re-review of alignment; it is not a harmless reporting edit when it changes what is rewarded or counted. Historical evidence keeps the definition used at the time.

The model does not prescribe one conversion metric, attribution model, universal return threshold, survey design or statistically sufficient sample. Those choices require the campaign question and Stage 8's complete test/measurement contract. It does require that uncertainty or unavailable downstream data cannot be hidden by a precise-looking dashboard number.

## 8. Scoped readiness and unresolved inputs

Brief acceptance is an attributable decision that specified inputs are sufficient for a specified next step. It is not a global `campaign-ready` flag. The following are decision checks, not replacement Pactwright lifecycle states.

| Requested next step | Sufficient brief evidence | What it does not permit |
|---|---|---|
| Scope the problem or request missing evidence | All 13 areas explicitly accounted for; unknown/conflicted items and owners visible; supplied facts correctly labelled | No unsupported objective/claim, accepted strategy or commitment inferred from the act of documenting gaps |
| Develop bounded concepts | Approved commercial objective/offer/segment and relevant binding constraints; usable audience evidence or explicit hypothesis; objective alignment; all inputs material to the selected scope resolved or explicitly excluded from that scope | No invented price/proof, production purchase, live audience activation, publication or spend |
| Request production or execution planning | The above plus exact required fidelity/output, current commercial/claim/brand dependencies, destination/measurement requirements and named unresolved prerequisites for later approval | A plan or request is not an accepted asset, implemented destination, verified tracking or permission to commit resources |
| Authorise an actual production, launch or spend commitment | Requires the separate authority and the full applicable later-stage production/media/destination/test/legal checks for the exact artefacts, configuration and conditions | Stage 3 brief acceptance alone never satisfies this decision |

Every unknown is evaluated against the action, not waved away because a later stage exists. For example, an unselected platform can be acceptable for concept framing that does not rely on its capabilities. It cannot be acceptable evidence for a launch requiring a particular hard exclusion. A missing price may permit research into audience language, but not acceptance of a price-bearing ad or an offer comparison that depends on it.

The bounded-scope decision must name both allowed work and blocked work, the unresolved item, owner and reopening condition. It cannot waive a governing requirement, claim an unprovided approval, or turn a real user-owned decision into an AI default. When an actual requested task requires such a decision, the operator stops the affected workflow and asks the owner. This generic model-definition stage does not request or fabricate that campaign decision.

| V12 | The decision permits only its recorded next step; concept or budget-planning acceptance never confers execution/spend authority |

The structural portion of these checks can later be automated, but many require semantic judgement or external evidence. Passing a field-presence check is not proof of truthful claims, representative research, lawful targeting or commercial viability. No executable production validator is installed by this stage.

## 9. Change impact, handoffs and bounded repair

Retain the accepted baseline and record a proposed change with its source, reason, affected scope, approver and outcome. Inspect only the dependencies that could change meaning or authority; preserve unaffected approved concepts, research and assets. A superseded source is not deleted from historical campaign evidence. This is a traceability requirement, not a manually maintained universal graph or a new orchestration service.

| Changed or missing item | Reopen/check | Preserve unless evidence shows an effect | Responsible handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business objective or economics | Advertising objective, success measures, allocation/commitment rationale | Factual research and assets not yet shown incompatible | Business Building/project owner decides; Advertising reassesses alignment |
| Segment/eligibility | Hypotheses, targeting mapping, market, related claim relevance | Other approved commercial facts | Business owner approves scope; Research/Advertising/executor inspect their layers |
| Audience evidence | Dependent inference and claim support; research-use validity | Segment approval unless the owner changes it | Research/evidence owner provides clarification; Advertising adjusts only the supported inference |
| Offer or price | Dependent claims, copy, destination, schedule and applicable approvals | Unrelated audience findings and brand assets | Business owner supplies revision; Advertising routes content and destination repairs |
| Market or schedule | Availability, commercial validity, language, audience mapping and delivery windows | The concept when its meaning remains valid in the authorised scope | Business/Legal/execution owners resolve their responsibilities |
| Brand requirement | Affected expression and asset use/rights | Approved offer, economics and unaffected evidence | Brand owner and specialist production; Advertising integrates |
| Claim/proof | Dependent message and expression, qualifications and use approval | Unrelated supported claims and selected work | Evidence/business/Legal owners; Advertising blocks or repairs affected assertion |
| Destination | Promise continuity, routing and event requirements; actual implementation checks | Approved ad idea unless the promise itself changes | UIUX/Software Engineering or destination operator |
| Measurement definition or configuration | Goal mapping, comparability and interpretation of affected observations | Approved product truth, prices and unaffected creative | Software/analytics/executor supplies evidence; Advertising limits conclusions |
| Execution capability or authority | Enforceability, authorised commitment and affected plan | Accepted commercial scope; never broaden it to fit a tool | Authorised executor, business/data/Legal owner as applicable |

The consuming project retains the full brief and decisions. Advertising sends specialists only the relevant exact requirements and necessary source/approval references, minimising private data exposure. Legal receives actual facts and the proposed use, not a demand to rubber-stamp a creative label. Pactwright, when present, owns lifecycle/Contracts; this brief supplies domain content and findings without requiring Pactwright for ordinary use. [Stage1]

Stage 4 consumes the accepted brief, intake claim/proof records, audience hypotheses, brand/offer boundaries and unresolved blockers. Stage 5 consumes the selected requirements at its production step; Stage 6 consumes market/budget/audience bounds; Stage 7 consumes continuity requirements; Stage 8 consumes objective/event definitions; Stage 9 consumes revision/context dependencies; Stage 10 consumes the exact market, facts, claim types, targeting method and source limitations. No later-stage implementation or legal conclusion is asserted by this handoff list.

## 10. Alternatives and design decisions

| Approach | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| Free-form narrative with no identifiable fields | REJECT as the sole contract: easy to read but allows missing price, approval, event meaning or audience boundaries to disappear in prose |
| Mandatory universal JSON schema and workflow engine | REJECT now: forces premature infrastructure and provider assumptions; this stage needs complete domain semantics, not a runtime |
| Readable Markdown with stable fields, revisions and references | SELECT: preserves completeness, human review and consuming-project ownership; later tooling can implement justified checks |
| One persona used interchangeably for customer, ad audience and platform segment | REJECT: confuses commercial scope, uncertain response and execution capability; prevents precise repair |
| One status combining evidence confidence, approval and readiness | REJECT: a convincing inference can be unauthorised, an authorised hypothesis uncertain, and an accepted brief not launch-ready |
| Copy upstream inputs into an independently editable marketing truth store | REJECT: invites drift in price/offer/segment; use referenced snapshots with explicit change decisions instead |
| Require every future execution detail before any bounded thinking | REJECT: makes the cheapest useful research/concept work impossible without increasing truthfulness; accept only a scope that does not depend on unresolved facts |
| Allow broad “approved” labels to excuse all unknowns | REJECT: destroys the meaning of approval; exact subject, action, authority, conditions and blockers remain necessary |

The selected approach is lean because it gives each distinction a concrete failure it prevents. It does not mandate 13 physical files, separate agents, an internal CRM, a customer-data store or duplication of Pactwright semantics. All 13 content areas remain mandatory accounting obligations regardless of physical representation.
