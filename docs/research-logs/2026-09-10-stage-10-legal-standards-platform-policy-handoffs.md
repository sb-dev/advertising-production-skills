## 3. Research findings and authority distinctions

The research distinguishes what a source can establish. CAP's scope separates its non-broadcast remit from legislation and broadcast rules. The comparable substantiation clauses are CAP 3.7 and BCAP 3.9; the latter identifies broadcasters as the duty-holder. CAP's AdviceOnline page explicitly limits its own authority. These findings justify storing issuer, instrument, clause, actor and interpretation separately instead of a single generic “advertising law” entry. [R01] [R02] [R03] [R04]

The ICO guidance distinguishes advertising consent from additional consent for intrinsically linked measurement, while retaining purpose, technology and supply-chain considerations. The US rule text and FTC staff explanations distinguish prohibited underlying conduct from presentation format and other obligations. Platform disclosure tools do not themselves prove sufficient disclosure. These examples justify exact-fact triage, not a universal consent or AI-endorsement verdict. [R05] [R06] [R07] [R08]

Google's policy separates category-specific targeting restrictions, applicable-law responsibilities and actual delivery limitations. TikTok's HEC scope is not a worldwide default; its France-specific announcement illustrates why a title month and update month cannot invent a precise effective date. Its review FAQ and Clearcast's procedure distinguish review stages and actual returned decisions. The Commission's accessible explanation adds platform-facing transparency/profiling concerns without supplying a full statutory review. [R09] [R10] [R11] [R12] [R13] [R14]

| Source/decision class | What to preserve | What it cannot automatically establish |
|---|---|---|
| Legislation or regulation | Issuer, jurisdiction, instrument/provision, relevant actor, authentic/version context, commencement/application and change history | Applicability to unprovided facts, an exception or a campaign-specific legal conclusion |
| Advertising code / standard | Issuer, medium/remit, exact edition/clause, relevant duties and status | That a code is identical to legislation or the same clause number applies across codes |
| Regulator/administrator guidance | Author, date, underlying authority, interpretation and express limitations | Binding legal force, a safe harbour or approval of the proposed campaign |
| Ruling / enforcement decision | Authority, actual facts, operative result, date, scope and procedural status | A universal rule detached from its facts, or a final outcome when only preliminary findings exist |
| Platform / media-owner policy | Product/market/category, terms/profile version, restrictions, execution and review conditions | Compliance with all law, blanket data permission, verified account support or guaranteed delivery |
| Specialist clearance / Legal conclusion | Issuer and competence/authority, exact request/facts, sources, scope, conditions and decision evidence | Unlimited future clearance, business efficacy, production acceptance or spend permission |
| Project / brand / pack requirement | Owner, purpose, accepted baseline, precedence and scope | Permission to override a binding constraint, fabricate evidence or change the business offer |

These categories are an original responsibility model, not a legal hierarchy algorithm. More than one regime can apply cumulatively. Where interpretations conflict, retain both, identify the affected action and ask the responsible Legal/policy owner for resolution. “Use whichever is newest”, “choose the strictest sentence” and “the platform accepted it” are not adequate conflict-resolution methods. A stricter project restriction can be a deliberate authorised choice, but cannot be mislabelled as the law.

Cheap professional preflight puts exact copy/claims, proof references, audience/data methods, commercial terms and intended surfaces beside the relevant sources before expensive production. Review the actual rendered/served scope at the later commitment, not only the earlier words. Clearcast is one researched example of staged review, not a mandatory universal service. [R13]

## 4. Ten-field context record

A context record has an ID/revision, owner, real/synthetic designation, requested decision, exact brief/offer/claim/variant/media/destination/measurement references, P01–P10, unresolved issues and linked decisions. It may be a Markdown table in the consuming project. Reuse earlier source, approval and change records; do not build a parallel legal database or maintain a universal graph manually.

Bind each assessed implication to its exact combination of jurisdiction, instrument, platform/product, category, claim, targeting method and time. Lists of markets and platforms do not imply that every Cartesian combination is covered. A shared source can support several scoped records without copying its text or making one approval global. Missing or conflicting combinations remain identifiable blocked dependencies.

### P01. Jurisdiction

**Required content.** Record the facts relevant to territorial/remit review: advertiser and other relevant entities, intended recipients/markets, offer fulfilment, medium, language, data-processing roles/locations where material, flight and transaction context. Separate the declared market from actual targeting capability and from a policy page's locale. Multiple jurisdictions or unresolved territorial questions must remain visible.

**Owner and acceptance.** The project/business/data owners supply facts; Legal determines the relevant legal scope. Advertising records the campaign implications and checks that a selected media route matches the accepted scope. A URL country code, English copy or the operator's location cannot alone establish all applicable rules.

**Failure and repair.** Missing advertiser/recipient facts or conflicting market boundaries block a dependent legal or launch conclusion. Obtain the exact facts or narrow the authorised analysis; do not silently enlarge the approved market. Preserve unrelated valid work. A US/Canada-specific platform policy cannot be declared a UK legal requirement without the proper source and scope assessment.

### P02. Advertising standard

**Required content.** Identify the code, standard or relevant regime by issuer, title, edition/version or captured text, exact provision, medium/remit, duty-holder and status. Link related legislation/guidance/decisions separately. Record whether a rule is binding in the assessed context, an industry requirement, explanatory advice, a recommendation or unresolved; that classification requires its evidence.

**Owner and acceptance.** Legal/policy specialists assess applicability and the code/media owner retains its own review authority. Advertising preserves the actual clause and its campaign implication. Similar rule names do not make CAP and BCAP interchangeable; preserve who must do what, not merely a paraphrased keyword. [R01] [R03]

**Failure and repair.** A wrong code, obsolete edition, unverified clause or guidance promoted to law blocks the affected conclusion. Correct the source/interpretation and recheck the dependent requirements. Do not replace all campaign work or claim blanket clearance merely because the corrected provision exists.

### P03. Platform

**Required content.** Identify the executor/media owner, exact advertising product/placement and market, policy/profile revision, advertiser/account category where relevant, permitted formats/targeting, declared special-category settings, review/appeal conditions and actual capability evidence. Distinguish policy eligibility, technical availability, configuration, review status and observed delivery.

**Owner and acceptance.** Advertising defines the needed conditions; the authorised executor supplies account/configuration/review evidence. Legal conclusions and data-use authority remain separate. A public help page is evidence of documented policy, not proof that a specific account has the required controls or approval.

**Failure and repair.** Unsupported or unverified hard constraints keep the affected execution path blocked. Select a different authorised treatment/executor or seek clarification without weakening truth or commercial scope. An appeal must retain the actual facts; disguising the destination, removing truthful category indicators to evade review or cycling identities is not a repair.

### P04. Product category

**Required content.** Describe the actual offer/product version, functionality, eligibility, commercial terms, regulated activities or claims under consideration and supporting business facts. Record legal and platform category assessments separately, each with source, owner, confidence/unknowns, conditions and affected jurisdiction/product. Multiple categories and interactions are permitted.

**Owner and acceptance.** Business/product ownership supplies truth; Legal determines legal classification; the platform's policy/review process determines its own category treatment. Advertising records their implications without treating a business name, feature label or category chosen in a user interface as a legal opinion.

**Failure and repair.** Do not infer that a budgeting app is necessarily a regulated investment service, or that “education”, “B2B” or “open source” makes all obligations inapplicable. Uncertain classification is a question with an owner, not an excuse to select the least restrictive dropdown. A real classification question needing missing facts blocks only the conclusion/action depending on it; this generic model supplies none of those project-specific conclusions.

### P05. Claim type

**Required content.** Carry Stage 4's exact explicit and plausible implied assertions, subject/version, population, market, time, units/comparator, qualifications, proof adequacy, rights and expression references. Identify relevant types such as objective capability, price/renewal, comparison, performance/savings, guarantee, scarcity, endorsement, environmental or other category-sensitive claims when present. Store the overall visual/verbal/sequence impression as well as component words.

**Owner and acceptance.** Product/research owners supply support; Advertising maps evidence and expression; Legal evaluates the legal question. Claim support, permission to use source material and permission to publish remain distinct. A genuine testimonial does not automatically substantiate a general efficacy claim; the exact assertion and evidence must be reviewed. [Stage4]

**Failure and repair.** Unsupported statistics, fake reviews, false urgency, misleading before/after depictions or a conditional promise shown without its condition block the affected expression. Narrow/remove the assertion, obtain evidence or request appropriate review; do not manufacture proof. Labelling an illustration or changing “will” to “may” is not automatic clearance of the remaining implication.

### P06. Targeting method

**Required content.** Describe the actual selection/exclusion mechanism: contextual or query context, audience criteria, first-party data/list, retargeting, inferred/lookalike/expanded audience or another specific method. Record data provenance and permitted purpose, affected sensitive/minor/category considerations, storage/access and profiling technologies, recipient/processor roles, notice/consent/other legal-basis questions, suppression/withdrawal handling, and each hard restriction's enforcement evidence from Stages 3/6.

**Owner and acceptance.** Legal/data owners determine lawful use; Advertising keeps the hypothesis inside the approved segment; the executor verifies controls. Consent, source access and technical upload capability are different facts. Platform-defined sensitive categories need not be identical to statutory special-category definitions. Contextual selection does not by itself establish that every associated measurement flow has no privacy obligations. [R05] [R09]

**Failure and repair.** Missing purpose authority, impermissible data use, prohibited discrimination/profiling, unsupported exclusions or material unknowns block dependent activation. Obtain the correct decision or use a genuinely different authorised method. Never reconstruct sensitive attributes or private identity histories to satisfy the record. Preserve the distinction between a control being required and actually enforced; a public policy statement is not delivery proof.

### P07. Policy source

**Required content.** Source ID, issuer, title, authority class, canonical URL or controlled location, language, exact provision/section and relevant meaning, version/captured representation, publication/update/coverage information, primary/secondary relationship, access method, limitations and linked interpretation. Retain enough permitted text or a precise locator for a reviewer to inspect what was relied upon. A hash identifies captured bytes, not their correctness or legal force.

**Owner and acceptance.** Research/source owners verify provenance; Legal/policy owners assess authority and scope. Use official issuer text where available; label secondary explanations and historical rulings. Record an instrument's specified authoritative language rather than assuming a convenient translation controls. For confidential advice, use restricted references and authorised review, not a public copy.

**Failure and repair.** A search snippet cannot support unseen exceptions; a community answer is not platform policy; a policy index is not every linked rule. Obtain the missing passage or narrow the claimed finding. An unavailable source required for the actual decision remains blocked. No invented clause, inaccessible full-text claim or duplicate-source corroboration is permitted.

### P08. Retrieved-at

**Required content.** Record the actual date/time of access with timezone and precision, actor/tool or method, source location/version, successful content scope, inaccessible portions and capture reference where retained. Date-only evidence remains date-only. A later retry, changed page or different language is a separate retrieval record.

**Owner and acceptance.** The operator records what was accessed; reviewers check whether it supports the cited finding. Retrieval establishes access, not publication, commencement, completeness or approval. A search engine's crawl time is not the operator's exact retrieval timestamp, and the footer year is not a source update date.

**Failure and repair.** Missing retrieval metadata, fabricated precision or a fresh date attached to an old unrefreshed extract prevents a freshness assertion. Re-retrieve where necessary and retain the earlier snapshot historically. A failed fetch must not be stamped as a successful read; the relevant decision retains its evidence limitation.

### P09. Valid-as-of/effective date

**Required content.** Separate requested assessment date, source publication/update, declared effective/application window, publisher coverage cutoff, retrieval time, specialist assessment-valid-as-of, conditions, expiry and review triggers. Preserve exact source wording/precision when dates are partial or absent. Record announced future changes separately from currently assessed rules, including transition provisions when actually established.

**Owner and acceptance.** Legal/policy owners determine applicability to the relevant act and date. An assessment says what was checked, for which facts and period, and with what limits. Retrieval after an announcement does not prove it is in force; a current-looking page can report a coverage cutoff before retrieval. A page title month or title-wide amendment date must not be invented as a provision's exact effective date. [R06] [R11]

**Failure and repair.** Missing effective-date evidence, a known coverage gap, changed facts or contradictory versions blocks conclusions relying on the unresolved period. Recheck the authoritative change history and responsible interpretation. Retain the old record as historical; do not automatically relabel all past actions invalid or all future actions approved. A review interval is a project control, not a universal legal expiry rule.

### P10. Campaign implication

**Required content.** Link the source finding and its assessed applicability to the exact campaign/claim/variant/audience/placement/destination/event revision, required or prohibited behaviour, disclosure/control, responsible owner, implementation evidence, acceptance decision, blocked scope and review trigger. Distinguish literal source finding, proposed design implication, specialist conclusion, implemented control and observed provider decision.

**Owner and acceptance.** Advertising translates accepted constraints into production and handoff requirements; Legal owns legal conclusions; specialists/executors supply actual output and evidence. The implication must be actionable: what must change, where, by whom and how verified. A vague “comply with policy” line is insufficient, but a structural validator cannot decide legal adequacy on its own.

**Failure and repair.** Missing mappings, contradictory disclosure, an unimplemented setting or inherited approval on a changed claim blocks the dependent commitment. Repair the responsible layer and rerun affected checks. Do not trade a hard failure against creative quality, conversion rate or deadline, and do not treat implementation acceptance as permission to publish or spend.

## 5. Time, freshness and conflict handling

### Distinct time facts

| Time fact | Meaning | Invalid substitution |
|---|---|---|
| Source publication | When the particular announcement/instrument was published, with observed precision | Retrieval date or an unrelated footer year |
| Page update | Publisher's stated revision date for the accessed page | Proof that each clause changed then |
| Effective/application period | When the relevant provision applies to the relevant conduct, as established by the responsible source/assessment | Title month, publication alone or convenience |
| Publisher coverage cutoff | Through which date the accessed collection says it is updated | A promise that no later change exists |
| Retrieval | When the operator actually obtained the identified content | Effective date or an invented timestamp |
| Assessment valid-as-of | The date/scope for which a responsible reviewer assessed applicability using specified facts/sources | Universal legal clearance or a page being reachable |
| Review/expiry trigger | A recorded event/date requiring recheck, such as a changed claim, market, use, right or rule | Automatic proof that the underlying law expired |

A valid-as-of record is a scoped assessment, not a freshness sticker. Before a commitment, ask whether the source, facts, effective period and actual implementation still match it. The recheck scope follows dependencies: new targeting method, new jurisdiction/category, materially altered implication, changed destination/terms, new data recipient, expiry/withdrawal of permission, publisher policy change or a relevant contrary authority can reopen the decision even one day after retrieval.

A future-effective change can require preparation now without being treated as the current rule. Where a flight crosses a verified change boundary, the plan must identify affected variants/controls and the necessary timely switch or containment; actual execution remains authorised and evidenced separately. Do not assume automatic grandfathering or retroactivity. When the source provides only a month, preserve month precision and seek the actual transition decision if an exact day matters.

### Conflict and uncertainty workflow

| Step | Required action | Owner and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Detect | Retain incompatible source passages, dates, facts or conditions and identify the affected decision | Advertising/Research records actual conflict, not a convenient merged paraphrase |
| Classify | Separate different scopes from a true conflict; identify instrument, actor, medium, market, category and time | Legal/policy owner assesses legal/remit questions; executor confirms product-specific scope |
| Resolve | Obtain the relevant authoritative interpretation, correction or approved change; record rationale and remaining limits | Actual responsible issuer; no newest-page or majority-source vote |
| Propagate | Map the resolution to exact affected claims, variants, audiences, destinations, tests and use episodes | Advertising and specialists apply bounded versioned changes |
| Recheck | Inspect returned implementation and required approvals; retain historical evidence and unresolved blockers | Relevant domain/Legal/execution acceptance remains independent |

An unreadable mandatory passage is an evidence gap, not proof of prohibition or permission. Bounded research or a safe text-only concept review may continue only when its own dependencies are sufficient and no user-owned decision is being invented. When the actual requested action needs a missing user decision, stop the workflow and ask. Generic definition of that path here does not itself require the user to approve a real campaign.

There is no universal rule that all sources must be less than a fixed number of days old. A stable authoritative instrument can remain useful; an undated live platform page can change rapidly. Record risk-specific review triggers and verify material current assumptions at commitment. Do not silently refresh every historical policy reference or weaken an inconvenient constraint to reduce review work.

H01 extends Stage 7's request/return, not a replacement approval system. A specialist can be a qualified responsible person or an actually available Legal Skills workflow acting within its defined remit. ## 7. Campaign implications, verification and change impact

### Implication record and production checks

Every actionable implication has a rule/decision reference, applicability rationale/status, subject revision, required outcome, implementation owner, evidence required, observed implementation, independent acceptance and blocked action. For example, an assessed requirement to keep a qualification audible is mapped to the actual spoken line and audio review, not merely a caption file. Stage 7 supplies the complete seven-dimension continuity model; Stage 5 supplies actual-output review.

| Decision scope | Required evidence | Not established by a pass |
|---|---|---|
| Source/context research | Accurate P01–P10 accounting, accessible relevant source, authority/time limits and open questions | Legal clearance or current account eligibility |
| Bounded claim/concept review | Exact supported meaning, approved commercial scope, known constraints and explicit unresolved production dependencies | Actual rendered disclosure, completed specialist review or launch readiness |
| Specialist conclusion | Actual request/return, source and fact scope, conditions and competent escalation when required | Unrelated jurisdictions, changed variants or unlimited future use |
| Production/destination acceptance | Actual output and relevant surface/modality/state checks implementing assessed requirements | Platform approval or publication/spend authority |
| Submission/launch commitment | Current applicable context, exact accepted output/configuration, required actual decisions and scoped authority | Guaranteed delivery, continuing compliance after change or business effectiveness |

Review material disclosure and eligibility cases at the intended surface: shortened/reordered assemblies, image plus implied claim, muted video, screenless audio, localisation, destination transition, unavailable offer, renewal/guarantee terms and relevant consent/withdrawal state. Coverage is chosen from the actual risk and justified, not an exhaustive arbitrary Cartesian product. Unknown actual output or control cannot pass its implementation gate by reference to a nice-looking plan.

### Change and smallest responsible repair

| Change or failure | Reopen and repair | Preserve where unaffected |
|---|---|---|
| Claim/proof meaning changes | Evidence, Legal/claim scope, dependent expression and disclosure; obtain new scoped decision | Other supported claims, unchanged research and valid creative parents |
| Commercial terms or category facts change | Business authority, price/CTA/destination, category and legal/platform implications | Historical approved snapshot and unrelated assets |
| Market, medium or placement changes | Jurisdiction/remit/profile, language, eligibility and actual disclosure context | A concept whose supported meaning still fits the authorised use |
| Targeting/data purpose or recipient changes | Legal/data authority, profiling/consent questions, exclusions and implementation tests | Approved segment and unrelated permitted observations |
| Source/rule changes or coverage is incomplete | Relevant effective/application period, affected decisions, conditions and change plan | Historical source capture and unaffected current permissions |
| Disclosure implementation fails | Exact wording/layout/timing/mix/state or control; actual corrected output and relevant rechecks | Supported claim and unaffected sibling variants |
| Platform review disagrees or rejects | Actual reason, category/configuration and source comparison; lawful correction or authorised appeal | Truthful product facts and the original decision trail |
| Prior conclusion is invalidated or permission withdrawn | Dependent use/approval and actual containment; Stage 9 scoped withdrawal/supersession | Valid independent evidence; no silent deletion of historical decisions |

If the responsible correction belongs upstream, Advertising proposes it rather than applying it. A legal or policy problem is not solved by increasing persuasive pressure, hiding the destination or replacing accurate terms with vaguer claims. A requested stop remains a request until execution evidence confirms it; residual exposure and liabilities stay visible.

Maintain source and decision history subject to actual retention/confidentiality requirements. Public logs contain reusable methods and explicitly synthetic cases, not private customer files or legal advice. No common legal database, universal compliance score, autonomous policy executor or manual universal graph is introduced. Pactwright remains optional and owns lifecycle authority outside these domain records.

### Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| One “compliant” Boolean versus scoped context and independent findings | SELECT scoped findings; a Boolean hides actor, date, source, variant and conditional decisions |
| Most recent source wins versus authority/scope resolution | SELECT explicit resolution; a new FAQ can be less authoritative or address a different question |
| Treat all jurisdictions and platforms identically versus exact applicability | SELECT scoped profiles and Legal conclusions; convenience does not resolve jurisdiction or category |
| Platform acceptance as legal approval versus independent gates | SELECT independent gates; account review is not all-law clearance or spend authority |
| Blanket AI-ad prohibition/permission versus exact implied claim and rights | SELECT exact-fact review; creative format does not prove truth or classify every use identically |
| Fixed source-age limit versus risk/change-based recheck | SELECT recorded review triggers and material current verification; age alone cannot establish applicability |
| Rewrite all work after any rule change versus dependency-scoped repair | SELECT affected-scope review and historical preservation; broaden only where the change truly propagates |
| Build a compliance engine versus lean handoff contracts | SELECT readable domain records and specialist/external execution; tooling choices remain for their own stages |
