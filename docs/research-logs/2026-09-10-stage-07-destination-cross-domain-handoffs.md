## 4. Destination and continuity records

A destination is the next experience promised by an advertising execution. It may be a web page, hosted form, app-store listing, app screen, marketplace listing, telephone conversation, physical visit or another explicitly supported route. A brand execution can deliberately promise no immediate action; that decision still requires review of any claims and disclosures actually conveyed. Do not invent a CTA to populate a field.

A journey record names each consequential transition, not just the final URL. Include relevant redirects, intermediary forms, locale selection, authentication, checkout, confirmation, fulfilment and post-install steps when they affect the promise. Record only the branches needed for the actual campaign; this is not a universal customer-journey graph.

| Record group | Minimum content |
|---|---|
| Identity and baseline | Journey ID/revision, consuming-project location, owner, `real`/`synthetic` context; exact brief, offer/price, audience, message, claim/proof, CTA, media-line and asset revisions |
| Route and states | Entry execution and placement; requested action; destination identifier and content/implementation revision; intermediate and completion states; supported locale/device/eligibility/authentication branches; known failure/fallback states |
| Seven dimensions | D01–D07 rows linking the source promise, destination requirement, exact observed or proposed content, evidence location, owner, findings and affected action |
| Delivery context | Market, language, channel interaction/modality, schedule, rights/approval validity, relevant profile and commercial availability/capacity constraints |
| Measurement interface | Semantic event references, implementation/configuration owner, correlation and duplicate-handling requirements, permitted data use and observation limits; no credentials or private customer records |
| Acceptance and issues | Requested next decision, inspected revision and environment, evidence coverage, independent findings, decision authority, conditions, expiry/recheck triggers and unresolved-item owners |

A required cell is populated or explicitly `unknown`, `conflicted` or `not-applicable` with its consequence and responsible owner, using Stage 3 semantics. Missing values cannot mean zero price, no disclosures or verified tracking. References substitute for duplicated content only when the exact relevant revision can be inspected by its authorised reviewer. Restricted source material stays in the consuming project's controlled location.

A route identifier is not an immutable content version. Record captured content/configuration and observation time where a third-party destination cannot expose a version. A returned screenshot establishes only the inspected view, not every interaction or branch. A successful response code establishes only transport availability, not truthful content or completion of the user's intended action.

## 5. Seven continuity dimensions

### D01. Message

**Record and acceptance.** Link the primary message, supporting priorities, audience hypothesis, selected concept and intended takeaway to each relevant destination state. Record exact ad expression, destination heading/value explanation and the permitted additional detail or omission. The acceptance question is whether a recipient following this execution encounters the same supported proposition and a comprehensible next step, not whether both pages contain a matching keyword.

**Evidence and owner.** Advertising owns the promise and semantic comparison; UIUX/Narrative or the destination owner supplies the proposed content and, when required, actual rendered journey. Begin cheaply with ad copy beside a destination content outline. At release scope, inspect actual content under the stated entry/context rather than treating the outline as deployed evidence.

**Failure and repair.** Bait-and-switch meaning, an unrelated destination or a reordered message implying a stronger benefit blocks the affected journey. Repair the erroneous content or route; return a genuine offer change upstream. Preserve a valid concept, other destinations and unchanged supporting evidence. Additional truthful explanation is allowed without forcing verbatim duplication.

### D02. Price

**Record and acceptance.** Link the exact upstream price and offer revision to ad, destination, checkout or other commitment surface. Preserve amount or approved price basis, currency, billable unit, charging period, upfront/recurring distinction, material fees/tax presentation as supplied, eligibility, minimum commitment, introductory/renewal terms and refund/cancellation conditions where applicable. A quote-based offer remains quote-based; a no-charge entry step does not make a later paid product free.

**Evidence and owner.** Business Building/project ownership supplies and changes commercial truth; Advertising compares expression; UIUX/Software or the transaction operator supplies actual commitment evidence. A side-by-side terms table is the cheap check. A checkout preview or authorised non-purchasing test can later verify display/behaviour without placing an order. Legal decides applicable presentation obligations, not this generic contract.

**Failure and repair.** Contradictory prices, hidden recurring commitment, unavailable advertised eligibility or a different guarantee blocks the dependent expression/action. Determine which surface departs from the approved revision; do not automatically change every surface to the latest observed price. Currency conversion and market-specific terms need an approved basis. Preserve the original baseline and reopen only affected expressions, routing, approvals and tests.

### D03. Claim

**Record and acceptance.** Map every material explicit or implied assertion in the entry, destination and relevant sequence to Stage 4's canonical claim and support assessment. Include stronger implications introduced by headlines, visuals, demonstrations, comparisons, adjacency or completion language. Record conditions, subject/version, population, period, comparator and permitted qualification dependencies. Destination additions are not automatically covered by the ad's claim review.

**Evidence and owner.** Advertising performs forward and reverse mapping; product/research owners supply evidence; Legal supplies scoped conclusions where needed. The cheap check annotates proposed text and described visual meaning with claim IDs and unresolved implications. Release review must concern actual delivered expressions and relevant assembled/personalised cases.

**Failure and repair.** A supported narrow ad claim cannot become an unsupported general destination promise. Block that assertion, narrow/remove it or obtain appropriate evidence and review. A disclaimer cannot be assumed to cancel contradictory main meaning. A stronger claim discovered downstream reopens the affected claim/proof review while preserving unrelated approvals and source facts.

### D04. Proof

**Record and acceptance.** Link the proof supporting each claim to its authorised audience-facing presentation and the place where the recipient can inspect the promised explanation or demonstration. Retain source/version, scope, method or sample limitations where material, date, access/rights constraints, and presentation conditions. Separate underlying substantiation, a permitted summary and a linked resource. Not every underlying confidential source must be published, but a promise to view specific evidence must lead to the promised accessible material or be corrected.

**Evidence and owner.** Research/product owners establish provenance and scope; Legal/rights owners govern permitted use; Advertising checks faithful presentation; destination specialists implement access. A source-to-presentation comparison is the cheap check. Actual-link/access review later establishes the material available under the intended audience context, not just to an authenticated administrator.

**Failure and repair.** Broken proof links, invented reviews, misleading chart crops, expired certification or a synthetic demonstration presented as a real result block the affected claim/presentation. Repair the resource, representation or claim through its owner. Preserve the original evidence; never fabricate a replacement testimonial or publish confidential proof to bypass an access problem. Permission to display evidence does not strengthen its evidential quality.

### D05. CTA

**Record and acceptance.** Link wording/cue to the actual immediate action, destination state, prerequisites, user commitment and defined completion event. Distinguish viewing details, downloading, requesting contact, obtaining a quote, booking and subscribing. Specify primary/secondary priority, deliberate absence of immediate action where appropriate, required data, any further steps and truthful confirmation/fallback messages. Success means the promised step is available, not that an attractive button exists.

**Evidence and owner.** Advertising owns action semantics; UIUX owns interaction clarity; Software or an offline operator supplies action and completion evidence; Business Building owns commercial commitment. A text walkthrough is the cheap check. At implementation scope, verify relevant validation, error, already-completed and fallback paths using authorised test data and non-destructive methods; actual purchases/messages require their own authority.

**Failure and repair.** A free-download cue leading to a payment subscription, a request form labelled confirmed booking, an unavailable phone route or an app deep link with no supported fallback blocks that journey. Repair the action label, route or implementation; seek an upstream decision for a changed exchange. Do not improve a CTA by removing informed choice, collecting unrelated data or disguising its commitment.

### D06. Tracking

**Record and acceptance.** Link execution/variant and media-line revisions to the route and Stage 3 event definitions. Specify event meaning, unit counted, qualifying/disqualifying conditions, event producer, timestamp/timezone, environment, campaign/variant mapping, permitted correlation, deduplication/idempotency expectation, value/currency basis where relevant, lag and observation owner. Separate delivery/click diagnostics, destination interaction, business-qualified outcome and external attribution output. A tracking parameter's presence is not evidence that a conversion was observed correctly.

**Evidence and owner.** Advertising defines the information needed and checks interpretation; Software/analytics/execution owners implement collection, routing and attribution computation. A proposed event-and-route map is the cheap check. Later technical acceptance requires evidence for the defined event, duplicates, failed actions, consent/data-use branches where applicable and relevant redirects or app transitions. Use synthetic test records; keep personal data, secrets and raw customer histories out of public artefacts and URLs. Legal/data ownership supplies applicable permission requirements; this record grants none.

**Failure and repair.** Broken campaign correlation, duplicate success events, a page view counted as payment, test traffic mixed with live observations or a changed event definition blocks the affected measurement claim. Repair the technical mapping and rerun the relevant tests without rewriting valid creative. Missing, delayed, modelled or unavailable observations retain their labels; do not infer zero, reconstruct individuals or promise cross-device linkage without evidence and authority. Tracking failure can block a measurement-dependent launch decision even when the content is correct. Stage 8 owns the complete test/attribution interpretation contract.

### D07. Disclosures

**Record and acceptance.** For each applicable requirement supplied by Legal, platform/media-owner or authorised project constraints, record exact source/decision revision, scope, required meaning/wording, surface, modality, timing, prominence/accessibility needs, language, responsible owner and validity/recheck trigger. Include claim qualifications, commercial terms, sponsored/creator identity, data-use notices and other category-specific information only where applicable. Record why an item is not applicable rather than using an empty checklist as clearance.

**Evidence and owner.** Legal owns legal conclusions; Advertising carries their exact implications across the proposed execution; UIUX/Video/Audio and technical owners supply corresponding delivery and accessibility evidence. A surface-by-surface disclosure placement table is the cheap check. At release scope, inspect actual reading/listening/interaction conditions, including shortened, reordered, sound-off or screenless cases when relevant. No single pixel size, word count or duration is introduced as a universal legal rule.

**Failure and repair.** Missing, contradictory, inaccessible, obscured or mistimed material information blocks the affected route. Destination disclosure does not automatically cure an inadequately qualified advertisement. Information necessary in audio cannot be assumed available in an unseen visual companion. Repair the responsible expression/layout/mix/interaction; return uncertainty over sufficiency to the appropriate authority. Brand preference, space pressure and a platform acceptance signal cannot waive a binding requirement.

## 6. Cross-domain handoff contract

Every request carries identity/revision, producer and recipient, purpose and requested next decision, exact approved inputs and source restrictions, D01–D07 requirements relevant to the recipient, fixed/changeable elements, requested fidelity/output, context/profile, acceptance evidence, unresolved prerequisites and resource authority. Do not send private evidence unnecessarily or replace exact claims with a vague request for approval.

Every return carries the request revision, actual produced artefact/configuration or a clearly identified proposal, accessible output identity/revision, relevant provenance, checks actually performed and coverage, deviations, unresolved findings, scope/conditions of any decision and the responsible issuer. A missing capability is an explicit blocker, not an implied successful delegation.

|---|---|---|
| X01 Business Building | Send exact commercial conflict, original terms and affected journey; receive a retained or revised authorised offer/price/eligibility/economics record | Business owner decides commercial change; Advertising rechecks dependent expressions, not customer strategy unilaterally |

| X05 Video / Audio / visual craft | Send selected concept, precise disclosure/action requirements, profile, rights and fidelity; receive actual requested output and craft/provenance evidence | Specialist accepts craft within scope; Advertising inspects actual meaning; text directions do not count as produced media |
| X06 UIUX | Send destination promise, terms, proof, CTA, required states and accessibility/disclosure constraints; receive content/design/interaction and continuity evidence | UIUX owns experience design; Advertising owns promise acceptance; design is not deployed software |

Advertising's integration review reconciles these returns against the same baseline. A revision disagreement returns to the affected owner; the newest timestamp or most permissive answer is not automatically authoritative. Partial returns may support a smaller explicitly permitted analysis step, but cannot complete a request that requires actual implementation. Real handoff instances belong in the consuming project; this repository owns the reusable contract and explicitly synthetic inspection only.

## 7. Journey coverage, readiness and preservation

Select coverage from the actual promise and context. At minimum consider entry placement and permitted assembly, mobile/desktop or screenless context, supported locale and geographic eligibility, new/existing user, authentication, consent/data-use state where relevant, available/unavailable offer, successful/failed action, and changed/expired terms. Each material branch is tested, blocked or explicitly not applicable with a reason. No automatic exhaustive Cartesian product or fixed sample count is required; the coverage rationale must expose omitted risk.

| Decision | Evidence sufficient for that scope | Not established by it |
|---|---|---|
| Review proposed continuity | Exact proposed expressions, seven dimensions, contextual route map and explicit unknowns | Deployed destination, real tracking, legal clearance or launch permission |
| Accept specialist design | Returned content/interaction representation matched to the agreed request and preserved baseline | Implemented behaviour, correctly collected events or final media |
| Accept implemented journey | Actual versioned output, relevant route/commitment/branch tests, current approved terms and applicable specialist decisions | Incremental business effect or authority to publish/spend |
| Accept measurement readiness | Defined events plus actual technical verification for required mappings and limitations | Qualified downstream results, attribution validity or causal effect |
| Recommend exact launch package | Applicable content, technical, measurement, disclosure and constraint findings pass for the same revisions; authorised owners resolve dependencies | Actual publication or spend; those remain separately authorised execution |

Use independent findings rather than a single readiness score. A `PASS` for technical routing cannot offset a `FAIL` for price. `BLOCKED` identifies missing evidence/decision and affected action; `NOT APPLICABLE` needs a scope-based reason. A complete generic model can correctly block a synthetic or real campaign whose implementation is absent; that does not make the model incomplete.

For change control, preserve accepted source and output revisions. Record the proposed change, reason, owner, affected IDs, fixed elements, approval consequence and rechecks. Price/offer changes reopen dependent copy, disclosures, commitment states and measurement values. Destination-content changes reopen the affected semantic rows even when its URL is unchanged. Redirect/event changes reopen technical and measurement checks; unrelated claim support survives. A proof withdrawal reopens dependent claims and expressions, not every unrelated creative. A localisation or crop that changes meaning is not merely a technical edit.

An unresolved released defect is routed for authorised containment as well as repair. Record affected executions/time windows and requested pause or rollback; only returned action evidence proves containment occurred. Prefer restoring the last valid authorised state when appropriate, but do not assume rollback is safe for changed commercial terms or fulfilled orders. Recheck the corrected actual output and dependent branches; retain historical unreliable observations rather than rewriting them into clean results.
