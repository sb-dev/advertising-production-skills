# Stage 2 source register: professional advertising practice

Research date: 10 September 2026. All 35 sources below were actually accessed on that date through public web retrieval. This register accompanies [the Stage 2 research and conformance record](2026-09-10-stage-02-professional-advertising-practice.md).

“Accessed” means the specific public text or abstract identified below, not every linked report, image, video, account feature, tool or downloadable specification. Source publication/update dates are separate from retrieval dates. An undated page is not assumed to have been updated on retrieval. No full PDF, authenticated advertising account, production generator, raw campaign dataset or private customer record was analysed in this stage.

The main record distinguishes source findings from original project recommendations. Professional associations describe craft and working practices; standards owners describe interoperability; platforms describe their own behaviour and market their services; original research supports bounded methodological conclusions; code text, explanatory advice and historical adjudications have different authority. None of these sources constitutes approval of an actual campaign.

## Search and access limitations

The search used the literal 22-discipline coverage list, then narrowed to professional bodies, standards owners, platform documentation and original research. Additional passes tested weak spots in outdoor, B2B, app acquisition, retargeting, experimentation and measurement. This is a purpose-bounded professional-practice review, not a preregistered systematic review or a claim to have exhausted advertising literature.

The public IAB digital-video measurement and standardised CTV measurement landing pages were encountered, but their full guides required additional access; they were not used as though read. The DMA Value of Creativity report landing page was encountered but its member-only report was not analysed; promotional multipliers were not adopted. Old standards-development announcements were not used to infer current product availability. Generic agency glossaries, search-result replicas of Google documentation, certification-answer websites and unsourced performance promises were excluded from the evidence base. Primary URLs were followed where search results surfaced third-party descriptions.

The Google Ads developer-blog release page encountered during discovery returned an internal retrieval error. No design decision depends on that inaccessible page. The current API documentation in R19 supports the limited app-objective distinction used here. The NBER retrieval limitation for R29 is resolved for this stage's bounded use by the accessible original researchers' institutional abstract, not by pretending the paper itself was read.

No mandatory Stage 2 source is named by the bootstrap. The inaccessible optional materials therefore do not block the researched practice coverage; they do limit the claims made from them. Detailed legal applicability, production-specification versions, account capabilities and vendor selection remain explicit subsequent-stage work rather than assertions in this research record.
