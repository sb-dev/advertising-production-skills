## 5. Cross-project interface review

### Business Building, research and Legal

Business Building supplies exact approved customer, offer, price, channel class and economic constraints. Advertising can vary the message or proposed audience hypothesis inside that baseline, not rewrite the exchange for a better metric. Spec 02's BR.C fields and AU.A layers retain this division. A new upstream family still being a bootstrap does not prohibit the actual business owner from supplying an approved record; it does prohibit pretending an uninstalled upstream skill executed. [S06]

Deep Research supplies attributable findings, methods, contradictory evidence and limits. Those can inform an audience or creative hypothesis. Research about what people care about does not prove the product delivers that result, and several generated statements are not independent corroboration. The knowledge states and claim-to-proof assessment in Spec 02 preserve that separation. [S07]

Legal receives the exact communication, material commercial and data facts, jurisdiction, date and source context. A generic approval label cannot replace H1–H8/J1–J6, and a reviewed script cannot clear a materially different image, claim or market. Advertising implements actual returned conditions but does not infer a lawyer, professional qualification or wider clearance from a skill name. No legal opinion or actual legal review was performed in Stage 21. [S08]

### Narrative and Video

Narrative owns relevant story/prose/screenplay craft. It is not a mandatory universal wrapper around every short ad string. A narrative handoff preserves the exact supported claims, words and material constraints while leaving specialist craft to its owner. Story canon, selected story alternatives and actual customer/product evidence are distinct; fictional dialogue cannot become a genuine testimonial. [S15]

Video explicitly separates campaign strategy from filmic production. Advertising's six production layers map to actual specialist artefacts through the request envelope, not by assuming its concept/master labels are a Video shot plan or picture lock. The production skill requires human approval at its stated creative gates. Advertising's preference-for-review therefore maps only to a development preference, never `approved` or `locked`. Source assets and optional scene/product/identity references must actually exist when that selected path requires them. [S12]

The current Video skill's negotiated production budget does not loosen its hard stop after two attempts on a shot with the same diagnosis. A third same-diagnosis generation cannot be justified by leftover budget or Advertising's generic retry envelope. A transient read retry is not a new generation; an uncertain billable job must first be reconciled by original identity. Missing provider peer skills cannot be disguised by implementing a custom provider client and claiming the chosen Video skill ran. These constraints already fall under Q03/Q05/Q06/Q08 and Spec 01's actual-capability/authority gates; no new universal retry value is added to core. [S12]

Video evaluation must inspect the property claimed: static staging is not temporal integrity, a thumbnail is not a complete sequence and a valid container is not coherent motion. Its actual preflight/tool prerequisites remain separate from Advertising's integration review. Node versions and helper tools documented for Video are not imposed on every Advertising instruction-only consumer. No helper or model invocation was executed here. [S13]

Video's public legacy progression includes four examples per level. That neighbour is a production-pattern reference, not an authority to change Advertising's exact three-per-level requirement. The family permits semantic equivalence in older projects and does not demand copying their every structural detail. Advertising keeps all fifteen original primary prompts and their actual-media obligations. [S02] [S11]

### Music and combined Audio

Music's `workflowState`, production policy and selected/approved/locked decisions are separate axes. Advertising's delivery/performance/evidence/fatigue/succession facets do not overwrite them. The handoff names the actual approved musical direction, relevant constraints, allowed changes and returned file/provenance/evaluation. A request for a small local correction does not reopen composition or justify regenerating every track. [S17]

The selected Music route requires its actual provider peer skills and model-native schema. Deterministic audio helpers have their own prerequisites. Genuine production stems and source-separated estimates must not share an unqualified label; transcoding and level processing do not alone prove professional creative mastering. Advertising's U03/U05/U06 contract retains those limits and the requested output fidelity. [S17]

The Audio README and bootstrap expressly preserve Music as the canonical music implementation until the combined family demonstrates migration/parity. Audio's wider Sound, dialogue production and integration scope is a target, not evidence that every such installed capability exists now. A source WAV is not an event/state/runtime audio contract or a verified integrated result. Conversely, a linear audio advertisement must not be burdened with game middleware merely because the wider Audio family supports that domain. [S18] [S19]

### UI/UX and Software

UI/UX's interaction contract carries actions, transitions, validation, errors, recovery and user control. Advertising adds its seven promise-continuity dimensions, exact commercial source and event semantics. A static screenshot cannot prove interactive states; a reachable endpoint cannot prove a truthful commitment or correctly qualified event. Software or the actual operator supplies implemented behaviour and tests at the requested scope. [S09] [S10]

A completed design handoff, transport receipt, deduplication check, qualified registration and retained/non-refunded outcome remain different evidence. Advertising may specify the measurement need and review returned interpretation, but does not build an analytics platform or silently deploy a destination to make its package appear complete. Missing implementation stays a dependency of the specific readiness claim, not a reason to discard valid creative. [S10]

Pactwright owns intent → decision → contract → brief → evidence lineage, graph mutation and lifecycle permission. Its standard pack currently declares delivery-specification, delivery-execution and delivery-review bindings to its named skills; that manifest contains no Advertising binding. Version 0.0.1 in a source manifest is not proof that a package was published or installed. [S20] [S21]

The central optional manifest contract is one-way discovery/compatibility information. It is not automatically the same schema as the inspected standard Agent Pack. Internal retained-quality analysis is not a platform targeting permission. [S22] [S23]
