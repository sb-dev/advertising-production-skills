## 3. Production semantics and shared record

Preserve the original sequence:

```text
campaign idea
→ concept
→ master message/script/visual direction
→ format variants
→ placement variants
→ production assets
```

These are distinct responsibilities and traceable records, not necessarily six separate files, agents or mandatory production meetings. For a small text ad, several records can share one document while preserving their meanings and decisions. For complex work, use exact referenced revisions in the consuming project. Do not make a manually maintained universal graph or new lifecycle engine a prerequisite.

A campaign idea is the overarching creative proposition answering the accepted message strategy. A concept is a particular communicable execution idea. A master defines the meaning and production direction from which adaptations are derived; it need not be one finished landscape video. A format is a mode of expression or interaction. A placement is its particular delivery context. A file/container is an implementation representation, not the campaign concept. Vertical orientation can apply to several formats; sponsored identity can apply to static or moving content. The nine research categories therefore overlap without becoming interchangeable.

Every production record inherits Stage 3's `real`/`synthetic` context, source and knowledge distinctions, exact revision references and scoped approval. Stage 4's evidence → message → claim → proof → expression → adaptation chain remains independently traversable. Production lineage does not replace evidential support.

| Shared field | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Identity and parents | Stable ID/revision, record layer, owner, consuming-project location, exact parent IDs and accepted brief/strategy baseline |
| Intended meaning | Objective/audience, intended takeaway and action; canonical message, claim/proof, qualification, offer/price and brand references |
| Content and fidelity | Exact text/sequence/layout/sound direction or actual artefact reference; requested and delivered fidelity stated separately |
| Adaptation difference | Changed and fixed dimensions, omissions, ordering, translation, crop, timing, sound and assembly controls; why the change is needed |
| Context/profile | Format, placement/product, locale, device/interaction assumptions and selected requirement-profile revision; unknowns remain explicit |
| Production dependencies | Required source assets, rights/identity, data/product versions, specialists, technical prerequisites and resource authority |
| Review and decisions | Findings tied to this revision and scope, acceptance/selection issuer and evidence, failed checks, repair owner and next permitted commitment |
| Reproduction/provenance | Relevant approved inputs, production instructions, provider/tool and settings actually used, returned output identity and limitations; unavailable details are not invented |

A profile records the provider/media owner, exact product and serving mode, market/locale, source URL and relevant passage, publication/update date if available, retrieval date, requirement version or captured scope, and a recheck trigger. Each rule is classified as a hard constraint, recommendation, capability description or unresolved condition. Account availability and configuration evidence are separate. A copied recommendation cannot become a rejection threshold without a reasoned project decision. A documented feature cannot become a verified permission.

No profile can override truth, approved business constraints or required Legal review. A hard requirement that cannot be met blocks the dependent production/execution route. The operator may propose a different treatment or authorised placement, but cannot quietly drop a qualification, enlarge the audience or invent a commercial change to fit a tool.

## 4. Six production layers

### P01. Campaign idea

**Definition and record.** State the organising creative proposition: what the audience should take away, how the campaign makes that meaningful, and why it answers the accepted objective. Record its brief/message/audience parents, mechanism, supported claim/proof boundaries, brand constraints, possible concept routes, cheapest representation and selection scope. It is not a new offer, media-buying strategy or a list of fashionable visuals.

**Decision and repair.** Advertising proposes; the responsible creative/brand/project authority selects within its remit. Selection permits only its stated next step. Compare the rationale and uncertainties before paying for production. If the idea depends on unsupported proof or unauthorised product truth, repair that dependency or reject the route. A delivery-format problem does not automatically invalidate the idea.

### P02. Concept

**Definition and record.** Describe a particular audience-facing idea within the campaign proposition: opening, communication device, main beat or visual relationship, proof treatment, action and expected interpretation. Link the parent idea and Stage 4 territory/concept where already defined; do not create a competing duplicate. Record alternatives, actual cheap representation, feasibility questions, rejected paths and the selected elements to preserve.

**Decision and repair.** A written treatment, rough composition or beat list can be enough to inspect the concept. The selection decision identifies mechanism and meaning, not unlimited approval of all later generated details. Cosmetic alternatives are execution variants unless their mechanism genuinely differs. Repair the concept if its mechanism fails the brief; repair only the treatment when an otherwise valid concept is poorly framed or timed.

### P03. Master message/script/visual direction

**Definition and record.** Define the semantic and craft source for production: exact approved message/copy or canonical wording constraints, script beats and voice/sound intent when applicable, visual hierarchy and brand/source-asset direction, proof/qualification dependencies, CTA and destination promise, and allowable variation. Include pronunciation, pacing or motion intent where material; mark irrelevant modalities explicitly rather than inventing a script for a static headline.

A script can pair time/beat, speech, on-screen text, visual action, sound and claim references. A static master can specify hierarchy and essential composition. A text master can define self-contained message components and forbidden combinations. Preserve source versions, rights, master-selection scope and editable versus fixed elements. A reference image communicates intent; it is not automatically licensed production material or product evidence.

**Decision and repair.** Advertising accepts campaign integration; the appropriate specialist reviews craft feasibility; the proper authority decides commercial, brand, rights or Legal questions. A semantic master may yield several medium-specific masters without losing the common proposition. Changes to claims or user commitment return to their owner; a local edit must not silently rewrite the master or historical approval.

### P04. Format variants

**Definition and record.** Re-express the master in a specified communicative form: static composition, assembled text, temporal video, card sequence, audio or another defined medium. Record parent master, chosen form/orientation/interaction, exact adapted content or actionable treatment, fixed and changed dimensions, claim/proof and qualification links, required specialist output, cheap test representation and format-level risks.

A format variant is not automatically a resize or a shorter excerpt. It may require a new sequence, different hierarchy, an audible explanation of visual meaning or a self-contained text formulation. A justified adaptation can omit an optional supporting topic, but cannot preserve an assertion while removing a condition essential to its meaning. Record an intentional change of claim or CTA as a change requiring its relevant approval, not mere formatting.

**Decision and repair.** Inspect semantic equivalence and craft feasibility before full production. Keep parent and sibling variants intact. If the communication mechanism cannot work in that form, propose a different treatment or concept for that form; do not falsely label an incoherent cutdown an approved equivalent. Stage 6 determines the full media-role rationale rather than this layer choosing spend.

### P05. Placement variants

**Definition and record.** Bind the format variant to a specific delivery situation: provider/media-owner product, inventory/device, locale, interaction/sound context, assembly or enhancement behaviour, dimensions/timing and current profile. Record exact adapted fields, overlays/safe zones where established, CTA route, destination/identity dependencies, configuration requirements and preview/inspection coverage. Unknown placement selection can support planning, not an executable delivery claim.

**Decision and repair.** Check the proposed content in the selected context, not only against a file extension. The authorised executor supplies account/control evidence; Advertising verifies preserved meaning and required constraints. A preview is evidence about the previewed configuration, not every device, combination or enhancement. Unsupported or unverified essential controls keep that route blocked. Repair the affected placement treatment or seek an authorised placement change without silently changing the commercial baseline.

### P06. Production assets

**Definition and record.** An asset is the actual delivered file, text payload, editable/source bundle or other output required for the chosen production task, together with its identity and evidence. Record accessible location, version and integrity identifier/checksum where available, actual content/type, producing tool/person, relevant inputs and instructions, timestamp, declared and measured properties, rights/provenance, parent placement/format/master, and review results. Expected filename, prompt, empty manifest and storyboard are not substitutes for a required finished asset.

For fixed media, inspect the actual image/audio/video or bundle at the specified fidelity. For assembled search/native/display work, the production deliverable can be an exact component set plus mapping, constraints and relevant rendered/assembly evidence; do not invent one universal “final image”. Distinguish produced components, provider-rendered output and verified live delivery. Receipt of one does not establish the others.

**Decision and repair.** Production completion requires the deliverable actually exists and meets the agreed request. Craft acceptance, Advertising integration, technical checks, required Legal/rights review and publication/spend approval remain distinct. A checksum establishes identity, not correctness. Failed assets receive a bounded repair request naming the defective output/revision; retain the accepted source, unaffected siblings and previous findings. No actual production asset is asserted in this Stage 5 definition.

## 5. Nine-format research and production applications

The following source findings are intentionally bounded; they are not exhaustive provider profiles. The project applications define reusable judgement. Production must verify its selected profile rather than copying these examples as timeless defaults.

### F01. Static

**Source finding.** LinkedIn separates image recommendations, technical constraints and device delivery; its vertical single-image variant is mobile-only. JCDecaux's portfolio allows motion on some digital products but not others. Static is therefore neither a synonym for print nor evidence that a chosen screen accepts animation. [T01] [T14]

**Project application.** Inputs are the selected concept, message hierarchy, exact proof/brand assets, claim qualifications, action and viewing context. Specify a deliberate focal point, reading order, credible depiction and the relationship between image and words. The output is a contextual composition and, at production fidelity, the actual exported artwork plus necessary source/rights references. A square feed image, a roadside poster and a display banner can share meaning without sharing one legibility assumption.

Use a rough layout at the intended scale/context to inspect hierarchy before commissioning detailed craft. For physical output, obtain the actual printer/media-owner requirements; do not invent colour, bleed or resolution defaults from a digital guide. Evaluate the actual rendered/exported text, image and conditions. A crop that removes a qualifier or a generated product image that invents a feature needs a local composition/source correction, not a stronger claim or a complete campaign rewrite.

### F02. Search

**Source finding.** Google responsive search ads assemble multiple text components and permit different orders. The inspected documentation limits headline fields to 30 characters and descriptions to 90; it also explains required-text controls. Those input limits and one preview do not certify every assembled message. [T02]

**Project application.** Treat the production deliverable as an exact, versioned set of text components and their mapping, with final-URL and claim/qualification dependencies. Input includes the approved query-context hypothesis, not a substituted business audience. Components must be individually intelligible and compatible in the combinations actually permitted by the selected configuration. Character-count checks need the platform's counting semantics and locale, not a universal string-length assumption.

A cheap composition review examines risky pairings, shortest supported presentations, omissions, repetition and the claimed action. Record what was and was not enumerated; sampling a few previews cannot prove all combinations safe. If a material condition cannot be reliably retained with its assertion, narrow the assertion or reject that execution configuration. Repair the affected component or permitted combination while preserving valid siblings. A valid text set is not an uploaded, served or effective campaign. Do not convert an asset-strength recommendation into a truth or approval test.

### F03. Social video

**Source finding.** TikTok's final inspected in-feed guide, labelled June 2026, distinguishes Spark and Non-Spark specifications. Safe-zone selection depends on dimensions, caption length and additional formats; the page warns that preview and device-specific delivery can differ. The separate identity guidance ties use of another creator's post to authorisation. [T03] [T15]

**Project application.** Define the opening, message beats, visual/voice/text relationship, source identity, rights, CTA and disclosure presentation. Plan whether required meaning remains available under the intended sound and interface conditions; “native-looking” must not become a fabricated customer experience. Requested outputs can progress from beats and rough timing to actual video, captions and appropriate source/edit references.

Use a rough sequence with explicit interface assumptions before expensive generation or recording. At production, inspect the actual opening and every claim-bearing moment, including caption overlap, crop, pace and any creator attribution. A platform permission does not prove the depicted experience or all underlying content rights. Repair the affected edit, caption, source or identity dependency; do not infer fatigue or performance improvement merely from a new cut. No template, creator authorisation or rendered video was obtained here.

### F04. CTV

**Source finding.** Amazon's inspected Streaming TV/Prime Video profile differentiates locale/supply durations and interactive versus non-interactive delivery. Non-interactive placements must not encourage clicking. Clearcast separately distinguishes script, rough-cut and final-file review in its own clearance process. Neither source is a universal CTV production or legal approval. [T04] [T12]

**Project application.** Specify intended viewing context, visual and audible message, timing, proof/qualification presentation, recognisable brand and an action the selected placement can actually support. A non-interactive call to visit or remember a destination needs an appropriate real route, not a click-shaped control. Where interactive overlays are used, retain their exact capability and obstruction constraints as a separate placement dependency.

Start with script/boards or a timed rough to expose unsupported visual implications and unreadable conditions. Request an actual finished file at the selected delivery specification, required accessibility companions and clearance references where applicable. Technical duration or sound checks do not replace claim evaluation. Repair the affected master/edit/encode or action mapping; do not transform concept selection into broadcaster acceptance. A programme-like render and a deliverable ad are different artefacts.

### F05. Display

**Source finding.** Google publishes separate specifications for responsive components and uploaded display files. Its responsive documentation describes component assembly and possible enhancements; uploaded image, animation and HTML5 work have different constraints. HTML5 access is not established merely by making a valid bundle. [T05] [T06] [T17]

**Project application.** Choose the needed control model explicitly: fixed composition, controlled animation/bundle, or externally assembled components. Preserve exact asset/feed revisions, price/claim dependencies, fallback meaning, destination and permitted transformations. A generated background or changed product feed can change an assertion even when the filename is unchanged. The return contract must identify whether it contains components, an executable bundle, rendered evidence or actual delivery evidence.

The cheap check uses the most restrictive intended layout and relevant fallback/assembly cases. Technical specialists implement or validate interactive packages; Advertising does not build the serving platform. Production review covers loading/render behaviour where required, readable content, motion/interaction and assembled meaning. A clipped CTA, broken bundle or unsafe enhancement is repaired at that layer. Disable or restrict an unreviewable transformation only through an authorised, verified control; do not simply claim it was disabled.

### F06. Carousel

**Source finding.** The inspected LinkedIn carousel product accepts 2–10 cards and does not support video cards. Those limits describe that product, not every platform's carousel capabilities. [T07]

**Project application.** Define the role of each card and the sequence-level proposition: introduction, demonstration or proof, conditions, and action where appropriate. Record card ID/order, text/artwork, claim/proof links, destination, required co-presentation and any optional/reorderable elements. Decide whether each card must make sense alone and which meaning depends on the sequence. Do not assume a reader will reach the last card to discover a material limitation.

A cheap card-strip review can expose repetition, unsupported progression or a false before/after implication before rendering. Actual production includes the exact card set and mapping, not a single contact sheet described as all delivery files. Check the selected executor's ordering and destination semantics rather than assume automatic reordering exists or is disabled. Repair the affected card or sequence/control rule; preserve accepted cards when their meaning remains valid. A video supplied to a static-only carousel requires a different treatment, not a renamed extension.

### F07. Vertical

**Source finding.** YouTube's Shorts FAQ recommends a 9:16 composition and distinguishes a three-minute allowance from a recommendation to use less than 60 seconds. TikTok describes context-dependent safe zones rather than one universal overlay. These findings concern particular products and do not make vertical a standalone channel. [T08] [T03]

**Project application.** Establish orientation and interface context alongside the actual form: image, motion, card sequence or native component. Recompose focal relationships, scale, type and action for the intended view. A centre crop may remove the evidence or alter a person's gesture; automatic reframing therefore returns a proposed adaptation for inspection, not a meaning-preserving guarantee.

An inexpensive tall layout or rough sequence should expose what survives the crop and where interface elements may compete. At final fidelity, inspect the exact output with relevant caption/overlay and sound assumptions. Do not impose a recommended short duration as an absolute eligibility rule, or infer desktop availability from mobile suitability. Repair only the framing, timing or supported expression at issue; a wider master remains useful source material when correctly identified.

### F08. Native/sponsored

**Source finding.** Google's Authorized Buyers workflow allows a publisher to assemble supplied assets into its own design. The FTC's December 2015 US guide discusses recognisable commercial intent across the initial encounter and destination. TikTok separately documents creator-post authorisation. Assembly, disclosure and identity are therefore separate research questions. [T09] [T10] [T15]

**Project application.** Distinguish a supplied-field native unit, a sponsored editorial treatment and a creator-led advertisement. Each needs an explicit advertiser/creator identity, authorised source material, truthful content, commercial-identification requirements and expected destination. Native integration is a presentation choice, not permission to impersonate independent reporting, create fake expertise or hide sponsorship.

Use a rough entry-point-plus-destination representation to examine the expected interpretation and required disclosures. For assembled units, request the actual field schema and rendered/context evidence; for sponsored content, request the actual text/media and entry-point treatment. Keep publisher-controlled styling as a dependency, not an asset owned by Advertising. Repair the specific disclosure, attribution, asset or composition problem and obtain the applicable Legal/rights decision. This model does not derive universal legal sufficiency from the historical US guide.

### F09. Audio

**Source finding.** Spotify's inspected Ads Manager/Direct IO guidance gives product-specific length and technical requirements, including -16 LUFS with a ±1.5 tolerance. Its suggested 65 words for a 30-second script is a recommendation, and a companion image appears when the listener engages with the app. These do not establish that a script is intelligible. [T11]

**Project application.** Define the audible message, pronunciation, voice/music/sound direction, timing, evidence and qualification wording, source/voice rights and usable response route. Treat an audio-only listener as a real production context; a companion display cannot silently carry a condition essential to an audible claim. Spoken and clickable actions can coexist only when their actual delivery conditions are clear.

Use a timed rough read to expose overload before commissioning production. Final acceptance requires listening to the actual file and, where relevant, measuring the selected technical properties, not counting words or trusting a synthesis prompt. Review speech clarity, masking, abrupt edits, claim/qualification pace and CTA accuracy. Repair wording, timing, mix or encode through the responsible owner while preserving approved meaning. A technically acceptable loudness value is not proof of truth, rights, accessibility or effectiveness.

## 6. Adaptation contract and invariants

The adaptation request identifies the approved source, unresolved question, target form/context, permitted changes, fixed meaning, required actual outputs and acceptance evidence. It also specifies the resource/production authority actually granted. Generation capabilities cannot supply missing source truth or permission.

| Change | Required reasoning and inspection | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Resize or crop | Check focal meaning, proof, brand, readable conditions and expected viewing scale in the actual result | Preserved dimensions alone do not establish preserved meaning |
| Cutdown or resequence | Identify removed/reordered beats, retained assertion/condition relationship and changed causal or temporal implication | A shorter excerpt can create a new claim; do not inherit approval blindly |
| Visual-to-audio or sound-off adaptation | Re-express essential information in the available modality; identify accessibility and response-route dependencies | A caption, spoken cue or companion asset is not universally interchangeable |
| Translation or transcreation | Preserve commercial terms, quantity, comparison, nuance, qualification and intended action; obtain competent review where needed | Fluency does not authorise a new market, price, audience or claim |
| Responsive assembly | Identify allowed components/combinations, mandatory dependencies, externally controlled transformations and evidence coverage | An unsafe combination cannot be justified by a safe preferred preview |
| Product/feed refresh | Bind exact product/offer/evidence versions, asset availability and expiry; inspect implied availability and pricing | A data refresh can be a substantive claim change, not merely technical upkeep |
| Creator/native reuse | Verify the particular identity, material and use rights; preserve required commercial identification in the new context | Permission to access a post is not proof of its claim or blanket reuse rights |
| Automated enhancement | Record the actual transformation/settings and inspect returned output or establish sufficiently constrained reviewed behaviour | Provider automation is an executor, not an authority or proof of semantic equivalence |

Google's Trim workflow explicitly includes preview and edit steps for proposed cutdowns before upload; this is evidence that the transformation and later campaign-use steps are distinguishable, not proof that its outputs preserve claims. [T16]

For dynamic output, the review contract must state coverage. Verify every finite high-risk combination where feasible; use explicit equivalence classes or constrained rules only with a documented rationale, assumptions and uncovered cases. Automated checks can establish structural constraints, not all audience interpretation. If essential safety/claim requirements cannot be verified or enforced, block the relevant configuration, constrain it through the authorised executor or select another route. Do not claim exhaustive review from sampled previews.

| ID | Production invariant |
|---|---|
| V01 | All six layer meanings and exact parent revisions remain identifiable, even when combined in one file |
| V02 | The accepted offer, audience, message, proof, conditions and brand remain authoritative; changes require scoped decisions |
| V03 | Every variant states changed/fixed dimensions and preserves or explicitly reopens affected claim/approval dependencies |
| V04 | Format, placement, orientation and file representation are not used as synonyms |
| V05 | Requirements have source, context and classification; recommendation is not automatically a hard failure threshold |
| V06 | Planned, requested, generated, received, evaluated, accepted and delivered are distinct facts, not inferred transitions |
| V07 | A required actual asset must exist and be inspectable; a prompt, mock, manifest or checksum alone cannot satisfy production acceptance |
| V08 | External assembly, feed refresh and enhancements have explicit controls, review coverage and unresolved-risk handling |
| V09 | Required meaning and disclosures remain accessible in the intended modality/context; unavailable interaction is not promised |
| V10 | Rights, claim support, craft evaluation, Legal/brand acceptance and publication/spend authority remain separate |

## 7. Fidelity, acceptance and verification

Clearcast's public process provides a concrete example of separate script, rough and final-file reviews. W3C's media planning guidance places accessibility requirements in production planning and distinguishes caption, description, transcript and player responsibilities. These inform the model's commitment and handoff distinctions; neither creates campaign-specific approval. [T12] [T13]

| Decision need | Cheapest responsible representation | Evidence before a larger commitment |
|---|---|---|
| Is the concept coherent and supported? | Exact text/treatment, rough composition or beat list linked to claims | Explained selection or rejection and unresolved support/rights limits |
| Can it work in the intended medium? | Contextual layout, timed rough read, boards or animatic when needed | Meaning, timing, hierarchy and feasibility findings at that fidelity |
| Is specialist production sufficiently specified? | Bounded production request and selected references | Required inputs, rights, output/profile and acceptance criteria; actual resource authority |
| Does the produced asset satisfy the request? | The actual returned file/component set/bundle and relevant source evidence | Craft, semantic, technical and context-specific findings tied to the returned revision |
| May this exact work be delivered or published? | Accepted actual output, relevant approvals and verified external configuration | Separate delivery/publication/spend authority and all applicable downstream gates |

Do not require every representation for every task. A short text production task may not need an animatic; a complex visual claim may require more than a script. The decision is whether the current evidence resolves the next commitment's uncertainty. A cheap representation is not permitted to answer a question it cannot establish.

Review the actual requested artefact and its context through independent dimensions: message/claim integrity, proof/rights, brand/craft, format/placement fit, accessibility requirements, destination/action continuity, technical integrity and appropriate authority. Record issue severity, affected revision, evidence, repair owner and rerun scope. A hard failure cannot be averaged away by an attractive visual score. Technical checks use the selected current profile; test-name presence or an encoder's successful exit is not evidence of semantic quality.

For text, inspect exact copy and supported assembly cases. For static work, inspect rendered composition at relevant scales. For motion, inspect the complete required sequence rather than only a thumbnail; additional technical analysis must state its coverage. For audio, listen and perform the required measurements. For interactive bundles, run the required technical checks through the appropriate specialist. This describes required future production verification; none of those media evaluations is claimed for this research stage.

## 8. Specialist handoffs and smallest responsible repair

| Responsibility | Bounded request from Advertising | Required return and acceptance boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Narrative/copy craft | Approved message, claim/proof limits, concept, exact action, voice and target form | Actual requested copy/script and dependencies; Advertising checks campaign integration, not invented proof |
| Visual/graphic craft | Composition/brand/source constraints, claim implications, fidelity and placement profile | Actual requested artwork/source references and craft evidence; rights and commercial decisions remain with their owners |
| Video | Selected script/boards, fixed meaning, permitted edit changes, sources, timing and output profile | Actual requested moving-image output, production provenance and evaluation; a storyboard alone is insufficient for final video |
| Audio | Approved words/pronunciation, voice/music rights, sound intent, timing and technical profile | Actual requested audio and appropriate evidence; a script or claimed waveform is not a recording |
| UIUX/destination owner | Exact ad promise, commercial terms, action and accessibility/interaction requirements | Experience/design continuity evidence appropriate to scope; design is not automatically deployed implementation |
| Software/technical execution | Authorised interactive/delivery package or instrumentation requirements and explicit interfaces | Actual technical artefact/configuration/test evidence; Advertising does not implement a CRM, ad server or attribution engine |
| Legal/rights/brand authority | Exact proposed content, source material, intended use, market/context and relevant prior decisions | Attributable scoped finding/decision; no vague summary or inferred blanket clearance |
| Ad operations/media executor | Authorised asset/component revisions, placement/control requirements, schedule and approval references | Identified delivery configuration and evidence; access or a plan is not spending permission |

The visual/graphic responsibility does not assert a new installed Production Skills family. It may be fulfilled by an appropriate project specialist or external tool under a bounded request. Combining responsibilities in one operator does not combine their authorities. Generation, rendering, upload, serving and other external execution remain external; tool/provider selection is not made by this stage. [Stage1]

A repair request names the failed artefact/revision, observed defect and evidence, relevant invariant, smallest change, fixed approved elements, owner and required recheck. A subtitle overlap normally calls for a caption/layout repair, not new claims. A broken bundle calls for technical correction, not a new concept. A missing real product preview cannot be repaired by fabricating one. A misleading master promise returns to the claim or commercial owner rather than being hidden in many smaller edits.

Record the replacement's parent and actual new output, then repeat the affected checks and any dependent contextual checks. Do not silently overwrite historical evidence or regenerate unaffected siblings. Pactwright may consume these findings under its own Contract/lifecycle model, but ordinary production does not require it. The central repository owns reusable models and clearly synthetic demonstrations, not private campaigns, credentials or customer records.

## 9. Alternatives and decisions

| Approach | Decision and reason |
|---|---|
| One finished hero asset resized everywhere | REJECT as a default: efficient only when meaning and context really survive; cannot solve unavailable interaction or modality-specific information |
| Independent native executions with no shared semantic master | REJECT: makes claims, price, brand and learning drift difficult to detect |
| Shared semantic master with justified medium/context adaptations | SELECT: preserves the proposition while allowing appropriate composition, sequence and craft |
| Highest fidelity before concept selection | REJECT as default: spends resources without necessarily resolving the strategic uncertainty |
| Cheapest representation capable of answering the next question | SELECT: makes commitment explicit without pretending a rough is a finished asset |
| Universal technical preset or cross-channel quality score | REJECT: different products, contexts and independent failure dimensions need their own evidence |
| Automatic conversion with inherited approval | REJECT: cropping, generated backgrounds, cutdowns and assembly can change meaning |
| Exact output/provenance plus scoped review and local repair | SELECT: reproducible evidence without a new marketing platform or mandatory graph runtime |

The model does not require maximal variant counts or production of every format for every campaign. The brief, media role and experiment question determine useful outputs; this stage's obligation is to research all nine categories and define a complete production process. Optional creative packs later may influence expression but cannot override the accepted business/claim/brand and legal constraints.
