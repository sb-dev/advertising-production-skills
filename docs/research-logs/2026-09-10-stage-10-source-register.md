## Research method and limitations

Discovery began with the literal Stage 10 context fields and earlier claim, targeting and disclosure contracts. It then compared UK code/guidance, a US rule and staff guidance, an EU institutional explanation, platform policy and a clearance procedure. The purpose is to establish the context and handoff model, not exhaust every jurisdiction, category or platform.

Primary publisher text was preferred to agency summaries, platform-community answers, search-result replicas and certification sites. Google Community troubleshooting was encountered but not used as policy authority. Meta policy searches did not yield usable primary text in this pass; no Meta-specific rule is asserted. Direct EUR-Lex access returned JavaScript verification pages; only the separately identified Commission extract is used for the limited EU point. No PDF, private legal advice, ad-account setting, provider submission or rendered disclosure was analysed in this stage.

A failed optional source does not become fabricated evidence. No mandatory named source is required by the original Stage 10 contract, and none of the generic model definitions depends on pretending inaccessible content was read. A real campaign whose decision depends on an unavailable source would remain blocked at that decision. Rechecking its selected current scope remains necessary; this research register is not a reusable compliance certificate.
