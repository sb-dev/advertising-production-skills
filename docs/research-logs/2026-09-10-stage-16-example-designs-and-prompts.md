# Stage 16: Fifteen progressive example designs and complete prompts

Date: 10 September 2026. Design revision: 1. Status: design complete, subject to the owning Stage 16 conformance record and publication gate. No example execution or installed capability is claimed by this catalogue.

Authority: [original bootstrap](2026-09-08-advertising-production-skills-new-project-bootstrap-process.md), Stage 16 and section 11. Selection, coverage, verification and evidence limits are recorded in [the Stage 16 research log](2026-09-10-stage-16-progressive-example-levels.md).

There are exactly fifteen primary examples, E01–E15, with three at each level. Each fenced prompt is independently copyable: it contains its own facts, requested outputs, constraints and evidence boundaries. The eight Stage 15 pack showcases are a separate suite. The `.example` addresses below are fictional destination identifiers; no real site, customer, company, approval or performance is asserted. Technical dimensions and limits are exercise profiles, not current provider specifications. A later live campaign needs its own current profiles and real approvals.

The examples request actual artefacts at their stated fidelity when executed. A design may deliberately request copy, a script, an offline representation or a production brief; it must not call those finished moving-image or audio assets. Where the prompt requests PNG, MP4, WAV or an implemented local HTML representation, an execution that returns only directions is unfulfilled. Real public hosting, paid generation, live advertising, data uploads and customer contact are never authorised by synthetic fixture facts.

## E01. Local inspection search unit

Level: L1 — bounded advertising unit. Skills: advertising-build, advertising-evaluate. Pack: local-service-lead-generation. Coverage: B2C, performance, search, claims/proof, audience eligibility, destination continuity.

Learning problem: keep a modest paid inspection separate from repair pricing and appointment confirmation. The cheapest sufficient output is exact text in a declared search profile; a campaign or generated visual is unnecessary.

Acceptance oracle: exactly three headlines and two descriptions; every assembled unit preserves the inspection/repair distinction; the CTA requests rather than confirms a slot. The source facts, not character fit alone, govern acceptance. Adversarial review: changing the CTA to “Repair booked for £25” must fail for both commitment and price.

### Complete copyable prompt

```text
Use advertising-build with local-service-lead-generation, then advertising-evaluate, for E01 in production/advertising/E01.

This is a SYNTHETIC teaching exercise. Everything below is supplied fixture truth only, not a real business, testimonial, approval or campaign result. You may create the requested local artefacts. No paid generation, external upload, publication, account change, customer contact or media spend is authorised.

SYN-E01-OFFER@1: Cycle Check offers one bicycle inspection for GBP 25 total, one-off. A written assessment is included; repairs and parts are not included and require a separate quote. Service is available only to adults in fictional service zones A and B, Tuesday to Saturday, 09:00–17:00 local fixture time. Two inspection appointments can be accepted per day. A requested appointment is not confirmed until the operator replies. No speed, safety-certification, savings or repair-success claim is supported.
SYN-E01-PROOF@1 consists of those supplied offer, service and capacity facts only. Brand: plain, helpful, no pressure. Audience hypothesis: adults seeking a local bicycle assessment; no evidence establishes its prevalence. Business objective: qualified inspection requests within capacity. Advertising objective: communicate the inspection and its limits accurately. No historical results exist.
Destination fixture: cycle-check.example/details, represented locally by destination-copy.md. It must repeat the inspection price, separate repair quotation, zones, hours and request/confirmation distinction. It is not a deployed URL.
Exercise search profile: exactly 3 standalone headlines, each at most 30 characters including spaces, and exactly 2 descriptions, each at most 90 characters. All 3 x 2 headline/description combinations are possible. These are fixture constraints, not verified live platform limits.

Produce brief.md with the relevant input/unknown/approval boundaries; claim-proof.md linking every material assertion to the two supplied records; ad-copy.md with the exact five strings and character counts; destination-copy.md; and evaluation.md inspecting all six possible combinations, commercial continuity and the CTA. Choose an appropriate truthful way to retain qualifications within the profile rather than hiding them in a destination alone. Record the actual audience hypothesis separately from the business segment and any proposed platform representation.

Use a request-to-schedule CTA, not a guarantee of repair or availability. Do not broaden the zones, invent reviews or optimise to clicks. Identify the qualified-request event and capacity guardrail without claiming a tracking implementation. Prefer a compliant expression for review; do not manufacture human approval or launch readiness. Report actual local output paths and any unsatisfied requirement.
```

## E04. A controlled search-copy comparison

Level: L2 — coherent creative test. Skills: advertising-build, advertising-evaluate. Pack: direct-response-performance-campaign. Coverage: performance, search, controlled variation, experiment design, downstream metrics.

Learning problem: define a testable opening emphasis while retaining one offer and a common response route. No observed data are supplied; a preferred idea cannot be called a winner.

Acceptance oracle: two complete three-headline/two-description bundles; only the designated first headline differs; all nine experiment fields are filled with an explicit feasibility gap where needed. Adversarial review: changing price or eligibility in one arm invalidates a hook-only interpretation.

### Complete copyable prompt

```text
Use advertising-build with direct-response-performance-campaign and advertising-evaluate for E04 in production/advertising/E04.

This is a SYNTHETIC teaching exercise. The facts and planning amounts below are hypothetical, not live evidence or spending authority. Create local text artefacts only. No external or paid action is authorised. Do not launch a test, contact anyone, upload data, change accounts or incur paid generation/media costs.

SYN-E04-OFFER@1: a two-hour online planning workshop with a downloadable workbook, GBP 40 total one-off, for UK adults. No subscription, certificate, guaranteed saving or promised business result. Capacity is 20 attendees per session. The next-step route workshop.example/details describes the same terms and a request to view session availability; no session is automatically booked. SYN-E04-PROOF@1 is the supplied duration, mode, inclusion, price and capacity record only. No genuine workbook image, customer review or performance history exists. Brand: direct and welcoming.
The business objective is qualified paid attendance within capacity. The audience hypothesis is adults considering a short structured planning session; it is unvalidated. The advertising question is whether leading with the time commitment rather than the online mode improves correctly informed registration.
Exercise search profile: 3 headlines of at most 30 characters and 2 descriptions of at most 90 characters. The fixture renderer always shows headline 1, either headline 2 or 3, and one of the two descriptions: four assemblies per arm. Arm A opens with time commitment; arm B opens with online mode. All remaining exact strings, offer, audience, destination and placement remain fixed.
Planning stipulations: propose a 50:50 independent eligible-person assignment, a 14-day observation window after assignment, primary qualified paid registration per assigned person, refund/complaint/capacity guardrails and retained attendance as downstream outcome. A five-percentage-point absolute improvement is the minimum useful effect for this fixture. Baseline response, reachable sample and delivery costs are unknown. Do not invent power, sample adequacy, traffic, a price quote or observed results.

Produce brief.md, claim-proof.md, variants.md containing both complete bundles with character counts, fixed-changed.md, destination-copy.md, test-contract.md and evaluation.md. Fill hypothesis, changed variables, comparison, primary metric, guardrails, downstream metric, evidence threshold, confounders and decision rule. State a suitable predeclared uncertainty framework and the missing inputs needed to establish feasibility. Separate design acceptance from permission and readiness to run.

Review every permitted assembly and the exact between-arm diff. No real winner can be declared. A design may be complete while a live launch is correctly blocked for absent measurement, sample feasibility and authority. Preserve rejected expressions and the reason; do not fill missing evidence with synthetic testimonials.
```
