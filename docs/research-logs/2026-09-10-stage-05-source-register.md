# Stage 5 source register: creative production and cross-format adaptation

Research date: 10 September 2026. All 17 primary sources below were actually accessed through public web retrieval during Stage 5. This register accompanies [the production model and conformance record](2026-09-10-stage-05-creative-production-cross-format-adaptation.md).

Retrieval date is not publication date. An undated living page is not claimed to have been updated today. A platform recommendation, technical constraint, capability description, account permission and legal approval have different authority. The model identifies the limited source findings and separately labels its original project applications.

Access refers to the public HTML text identified below. No linked full PDF, safe-zone image, ZIP template, embedded audio/video, authenticated advertising account or production generator was analysed or executed. A directory or documentation link is not evidence that the underlying tool, artefact or permission was accessed.

## Research method and limits

The research began with the literal nine-format list, then followed primary technical and production guidance to investigate assembly, context, interaction, fidelity, rights and accessibility. The final coverage is static, search, social video, CTV, display, carousel, vertical, native/sponsored and audio. The categories overlap; coverage does not imply nine isolated channels or a universal technical profile.

Repeated retrieval returned differently dated or differently detailed variants of some living pages. The final inspected TikTok in-feed text explicitly says June 2026; earlier retrieved text said March. The final Amazon and LinkedIn tables also differed in details from earlier retrieval. The record uses only the final relevant text for its limited current examples, omits unstable or unnecessary numeric claims, and does not assert that a platform changed during this session. These are reasons to capture source/profile revisions at actual production, not reasons to treat an earlier snapshot as present account truth. Earlier accepted research remains a historical record and is not silently rewritten.

IAB Tech Lab's public native-standard landing page was encountered, but its linked full standards were not analysed or implemented. Search results for new native-ad research, promotional case studies and older video-performance guidance did not become evidence of mandatory architecture, current product availability or advertising effectiveness. The directly accessed primary documentation supplies the bounded format findings needed here.

The source set is not an exhaustive all-provider specification catalogue, legal audit or tested integration matrix. The bootstrap requires research into every named format, not production on every vendor. Provider selection, execution boundaries and current legal applicability remain their own stages. No mandatory Stage 5 source or model requirement is unresolved. Actual consumer projects must obtain their exact selected-placement requirements, source rights, account capabilities and approvals before relying on them for execution.
