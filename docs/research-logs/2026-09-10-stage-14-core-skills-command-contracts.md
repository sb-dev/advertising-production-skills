| Duplicate mutable rules or a mandatory live reference server | REJECT | Either drifts commercial/evidence constraints or makes simple local work depend on unnecessary infrastructure |

## 4. Common contract and composition

### 4.1 Invocation and returned work

A command is an intent executed by the existing capable host under the selected skill. Portable invocation is, for example, **“Use advertising-build to design-test for campaign C, using these approved inputs, and write the complete test contract.”** Command names are not binaries, API endpoints or shell scripts. Do not invent `advertising-build` terminal commands or assume a slash alias exists.

Every invocation identifies the requested decision, consuming-project location, subject/revision and real/synthetic context; relevant approved source and current artefact references; scope/fidelity; fixed versus permitted changes; necessary data/recipient/resource authority; and required outputs/acceptance evidence. Read existing project conventions and supplied records first. Missing required inputs retain the Stage 3 knowledge state, owner, blocked action and needed evidence. Do not ask again for information or exact authority already present.

Every return identifies the request and inspected inputs, actual output locations/revisions, findings with supporting evidence and coverage, known deviations/unknowns, declared changes, unaffected work, relevant actual execution receipts and the next permitted decision/owner. It states whether the requested work was fulfilled. Findings use PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or justified NOT APPLICABLE. A correctly completed audit can return a FAIL for an unsupported advertisement; that does not turn the advertisement into accepted work. A command requesting finished media is not fulfilled by returning only a brief and labelling it a successful draft.

The shared contract can be represented in Markdown or an existing project record; no new universal schema service or fixed number of campaign files is required. Keep source data, operational status, evaluative finding and owner approval distinct. A review document is an actual review artefact, not actual media, campaign delivery or market evidence.

### 4.2 Domain semantics that every skill preserves

| Model responsibility | Required retained meaning |
|---|---|
| Brief and audience | All 13 Stage 3 areas; business segment, audience hypothesis and platform representation remain separate; knowledge and use-decision states are independent |
| Strategy | Message hierarchy, claim inventory, proof inventory, objection map, territories, hook/angle matrix, CTA architecture and creative hypotheses; exact evidence-to-adaptation chain |
| Production | Idea → concept → master → format → placement → actual asset; fixed/changed dimensions, requested versus delivered fidelity, actual file/component and independent craft/integration evidence; static, search, social video, CTV, display, carousel, vertical, native/sponsored and audio remain covered |
| Media | All 11 Stage 6 dimensions and B01 budget; forecast/configuration/observation distinctions, enforcement limits and disjoint exposure accounting |
| Journey | All seven Stage 7 continuity dimensions; exact request/return and relevant states, content versus deployed behaviour and action versus completed commitment |
| Experiment and measurement | All nine Stage 8 test fields; source/unit/window/maturity/unknowns and verified downstream versus platform versus causal quantities |
| Learning and state | Seven Stage 9 records and five facets: delivery, performance assessment, evidence validity, fatigue finding and succession; actual transitions and withdrawn conclusions preserve history |
| Policy and authority | Ten Stage 10 context fields, exact combination/time applicability and Legal-owned conclusions; approval binds issuer, subject/revision, action, time and conditions |

These are the existing models, not competing definitions. The applicable full model must be available in the skill's payload or exact authorised project reference. A skill may load only the relevant subject sections, but cannot omit a required gate because the rest of the source is long.

### 4.3 Authority, preservation and delegation

Apply Stage 12's ordered gates: scope/baseline → actual capability/effects → authority/safe exposure → bounded invocation → actual return/verification → decision/preservation. Local creation, external reads, disclosures/uploads, paid generation, configuration/activation/spend and destructive/irreversible effects remain distinct. Tool access, allowed-tools metadata, a Boolean approval field, successful validation or a creative preference does not grant authority.

Keep account administration, rendering, campaign upload, bidding/spend, ad serving, collection, conversion tracking, attribution computation and hosting external. Advertising can write bounded text directly. Specialist craft or implementation is requested with the exact Stage 12 Q01–Q08 fields and returned U01–U07 evidence. One operator may perform multiple responsibilities without combining their approvals. A missing specialist/tool is recorded accurately; a supposed handoff cannot be reported as executed without actual return evidence.

A requested repair identifies the defective subject/revision, evidence, smallest change, fixed elements, owner and rechecks. Use new linked revisions; do not overwrite accepted parents or unrelated siblings. Compare actual before/after outputs, reopen only affected approvals and dependent checks, and retain invalid/inconclusive evidence with its limitations. A new concept or commercial term is not disguised as a local refresh.

For provider/timeouts, preserve request IDs and unknown outcomes; reconcile state and costs before another possibly billable or mutating call. Retries, concurrency and substitutions stay inside exact authority. A new recipient, cost, subject or coupled effect needs the owning decision when not already covered. Actual permission denial is distinct from a missing discovery entry. Do not request permission resets without evidence.

### 4.4 Resource and installation design boundary

Each of the four selected skills has its own SKILL.md and directly linked, task-focused resources. Each independently installed payload must contain all common constraints and command-specific references needed for its own work. Core invariants have one canonical authoring source; repeated payload copies are derived, version-bound and checked for consistency rather than edited separately. The exact source/build layout and any justified synchronisation helper belong to the repository contracts and implementation stages.

No installed reference may rely on an absent sibling skill, an absolute developer path or private bootstrap research. Handoff recipients may be optional; when absent, produce a bounded request or direct work already inside the active skill's authority, never a fake invocation. An evaluator's complete criteria must be available without installing the build skill. Installing pack-author must not silently install advertising accounts or an execution runtime. Provider integrations are opt-in task dependencies, not required frontmatter imports.

Use the required `name` and `description` fields and only justified optional metadata. Names match their eventual directories. Keep the main instruction body within the standard's recommended concise range; make relevant resource links direct and readable. Do not set a broad allowed-tools field as a security substitute. Format checks and clean installation are necessary later proofs, not performed by this design document. [S01] [S02]

## 5. Four complete skill contracts

### S1. advertising-build

**Activation and exclusion.** Use for new campaign work, brief/strategy/concept creation, format adaptation, media and test planning or assembling a launch package. Read available upstream context before asking. Existing-performance diagnosis routes to optimise; an explicit audit routes to evaluate; reusable pack authoring routes to pack-author. A small bounded text task does not trigger the entire campaign chain.

**Inputs and operation.** Read the invocation envelope and relevant models; identify the next decision and required fidelity, distinguish supplied facts from hypotheses, and resolve blocked commercial/claim dependencies. Perform only the requested creation scope. Where comparison is needed, produce actual alternative representations and explain selection. Route specialist production only with exact permitted inputs/authority and inspect actual required returns. Apply appropriate independent evaluation criteria before claiming fulfilment.

**Outputs and lifecycle.** Produces versioned briefs/audience/strategy, exact concepts/copy/components, production/media/test requests or launch manifests as required by its 13 commands. Record what was generated, selected, evaluated and actually approved separately. It may recommend or record a preference for review; only attributable scoped authority supports approved-for-scope. No draft, launch manifest or file receipt makes delivery active.

**Repair and verification.** Correct its own failed expression or plan within the requested scope, preserving fixed facts and siblings. Return commercial, Legal, data or specialist defects to the owning responsibility. Check all command-specific completeness, semantic and evidence gates; when the requested final output is absent, report unfulfilled/blocked rather than relabel the request. A favourable audit never auto-activates a campaign.

**Proposed metadata description:** Builds evidence-led campaign briefs, message and claim-proof maps, concepts, format adaptations, media plans, experiments and launch packages from approved business inputs. Use for new advertising or bounded creative planning and production requests; not for autonomous spend or legal clearance.

### S2. advertising-optimise

**Activation and exclusion.** Use for analysing supplied campaign results, diagnosing performance or delivery issues, investigating fatigue, proposing the next test, bounded refresh and preserving learning. An instruction to improve performance is not authority to change the offer, customer or budget. With no reliable observations, inspect available content and evidence gaps; do not invent a market diagnosis.

**Inputs and operation.** Read exact baseline, observations, context, change/exposure history and existing decisions. Reconcile measurement before efficacy claims. Consider all seven responsible failure layers and plausible confounders; select the smallest evidence-producing or corrective step. Creation can be handed to build with a bounded repair brief; execution remains with the relevant specialist/operator. Use the same core contracts rather than maintain alternative versions inside optimise.

**Outputs and lifecycle.** Produces source-separated results, contextual diagnoses, complete next-test contracts, bounded corrections with actual requested outputs, and learning/state events. Keep observed result, operational explanation and causal assertion separate. Preserve all five state facets: a historical winner can later be fatigued or its conclusion withdrawn; invalid evidence is not automatically a losing concept.

**Repair and verification.** Check validity, maturity, downstream guardrails, authority and before/after preservation. Failed evidence is retained with the proper scope. A refresh request is not adopted supersession; actual activation/pausing needs executor returns. Unknown state/cost after a request requires reconciliation before retry. Optimise does not run an always-on detector, release reserve or apply account changes on its own.

**Proposed metadata description:** Interprets advertising results, diagnoses delivery, audience, creative, placement and destination failures, investigates fatigue, designs next tests and directs bounded repairs while preserving campaign learning. Use for existing campaign improvement; does not infer spending authority or causal success from platform metrics.

### S3. advertising-evaluate

**Activation and exclusion.** Use for a requested audit, readiness check, claim/proof review, experiment or measurement validation, preservation comparison or failure triage. It can evaluate proposed text or actual final media, but must name the fidelity and evidence it actually inspected. It does not silently rewrite work merely because a review found a defect.

**Inputs and operation.** Bind criteria to the exact request/subject/revision, original constraints and actual supporting outputs. Select the applicable independent evaluation dimensions, inspect the relevant evidence and report omitted/blocked coverage. Use deterministic checks only for their proper properties and substantive review for meaning, craft and reasoning. Legal conclusions and business approvals remain external.

**Outputs and lifecycle.** Produces an evaluation with scope, criteria, actual inspected artefacts, dimension-level findings/severity, supporting evidence, uncertainty and bounded repair owner. Keeps request fulfilment separate from subject acceptance. No aggregate score offsets a hard failure. Evaluation findings do not themselves update commercial truth, activate delivery or create owner approval.

**Repair and verification.** Audit its own coverage and citations before returning. Repair an inaccurate finding with a new attributable revision; preserve the original history where permitted. To change a failed campaign output, supply an exact repair request to build/optimise or its specialist owner, and inspect the actual replacement when requested. Tests of evaluator behaviour must include correctly detected failures, not only attractive passing examples.

**Proposed metadata description:** Audits advertising claims, proof, format fit, destination continuity, experiment validity, measurement, policy context and preservation. Use for independent campaign or artefact evaluation and failure triage; reports scoped evidence and blockers without a universal quality score or implied legal approval.

### S4. advertising-pack-author

**Activation and exclusion.** Use to create or revise a reusable advertising campaign grammar, not an individual campaign or platform API profile. Read existing core/pack contracts and evidence before adding another pack. A one-off request or repeated platform name alone does not justify an extension.

**Inputs and operation.** Bind the authoring need to independently useful behaviour, core compatibility, approved constraints, production context and allowed variation. Define how the grammar changes emphasis, structure or decision priorities while leaving common truth, authority and repair rules in core. Compare overlapping candidates and reject duplication. Specify exact showcase inputs/prompt and controlled positive/negative/differential evidence required for its requested maturity.

**Outputs and lifecycle.** Produces the proposed pack contract, authoring decision/revision, compatibility/precedence statement, full showcase prompt, evaluation cases and comparison specification or actual requested evidence. Authoring can be complete at design scope without claiming maturity. A mature pack requires the actual showcase and executed behavioural/negative and core-versus-pack evidence; a proposed comparison is not that result.

**Repair and verification.** Reject or repair core overrides, unsupported default facts, hidden data/spend effects, platform-only wrappers and tests that merely check filenames. Revisions identify changed grammar and rerun affected compatibility/differential checks at their required scope. Existing accepted campaigns and unrelated packs remain untouched. Stage 15 defines the detailed authoring/candidate catalogue on this complete skill boundary.

**Proposed metadata description:** Authors and revises bounded advertising Extension Packs with explicit core compatibility, constraint precedence, showcase prompts and behavioural, negative and core-versus-pack evaluation requirements. Use for reusable campaign grammars, not platform wrappers, individual campaigns or authority overrides.

### C01. frame-campaign

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 3; Stage 1 boundaries.

**Inputs.** Requested advertising change, available project context and the requested decision/fidelity. Reuse existing campaign records before requesting new facts.

**Operation and outputs.** Identify the business-to-advertising question, owner, scope, supplied versus missing brief areas, outputs, evidence needs and next commitment. Produce a versioned brief frame referencing all thirteen intake areas and three audience layers; unresolved values retain their knowledge state.

**Checks and failure.** Check domain scope, objective and blocked dependencies. A frame may correctly identify unknowns, but cannot be called an accepted complete launch brief. Refer commercial strategy or genuinely required missing input to its owner; never invent a price or default market.

### C02. ingest-business-brief

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 3; Stage 7 X01.

**Inputs.** Exact accessible business objective, approved offer/price/segment/channel/economics records, their authority and use restrictions, plus the current campaign brief if present.

**Operation and outputs.** Map upstream facts into the thirteen-area brief without duplicating editable truth. Preserve source revisions, knowledge, validity and use decisions; reconcile contradictions by authority, not timestamp. Return the populated intake and a dependency/issue list.

**Checks and failure.** Check each imported value against its actual source and approval scope. Unknown price is not zero. A changed exchange or commercial audience cannot be accepted by Advertising; the affected downstream work remains blocked until the responsible owner resolves it.

### C03. define-objective

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 3 C01/C02/C08/C13; Stage 6 M08.

**Inputs.** Business outcome, economic/capacity guardrails, audience and horizon; available event or study definitions with their implementation status.

**Operation and outputs.** Define the intended advertising-induced change, primary indicator, causal rationale, priority/conflict rule and relationship to qualified downstream value. Separately map any proposed platform goal and identify proxy gaps. Return objective and measurement-requirement records.

**Checks and failure.** Check units, population, horizon and whether improving the proxy would reward the wrong behaviour. Do not manufacture targets or promise platform access. Unresolved economic limits prohibit profitable-scaling claims, not truthful bounded objective analysis.

### C04. model-audience

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 3 A01–A03; Stage 6 M03/M04/M10.

**Inputs.** Approved business segment, audience evidence with limitations, intended contexts, exclusions and a selected execution profile only when one exists.

**Operation and outputs.** Retain separate business-segment, advertising-hypothesis and platform-representation records. Explain the response hypothesis, contradictory evidence and a possible observation. Map each hard rule to enforced, signal-only, unsupported or unverified with actual evidence and limits.

**Checks and failure.** Check hypothesis scope, data-use authority, expansion behaviour and every hard exclusion. Signal-only or unverified enforcement cannot support an executable constrained audience. Seek a permitted mapping or upstream decision; do not broaden the segment to fit a convenient tool.

### C05. build-message-map

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 4 M01/M04/M07/M08.

**Inputs.** Accepted brief/objective/audience, supported claims and proof, brand constraints, documented or explicitly hypothesised objections and the intended action.

**Operation and outputs.** Create primary/supporting message hierarchy, objection responses and unanswered limitations, CTA semantics and message/creative hypotheses. Link each meaning to its audience and support. Additional explanation may vary by context without changing the approved offer.

**Checks and failure.** Check priority, truthful objection handling and claim/action consistency. A real product limitation is acknowledged or returned upstream, not repaired with invented urgency or a guarantee. Missing support blocks the dependent message expression, not unrelated valid messages.

### C06. build-claim-proof-map

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 4 M02/M03 and evidence chain; Stage 10.

**Inputs.** Exact proposed explicit/implied assertions, product/offer versions, source evidence, relevant qualifiers, access/rights limits and existing decisions.

**Operation and outputs.** Create claim and proof inventories and reasoned support links: adequate, partial, incompatible, unverified or obsolete for the stated scope. Record supported and unsupported meanings, contradictions, permitted presentation and responsible review. Preserve business evidence → hypothesis → claim → proof → expression → adaptation.

**Checks and failure.** Inspect compound and implied claims, populations, comparators, dates and permissions separately. A generated testimonial or mock chart is not real substantiation. Block, narrow or remove the assertion or obtain appropriate evidence; do not mark Legal clearance from this mapping.

### C07. generate-creative-territories

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 4 M05/M06; Stage 5 P01/P02.

**Inputs.** Accepted message/claim/proof/CTA baseline, audience/context and brand constraints; requested breadth, effort and decision scope.

**Operation and outputs.** Develop genuinely different communication mechanisms with actual short treatments, proof dependencies, cheap representations and feasibility questions. Include a hook/angle matrix with actual opening expressions, cell IDs, claim/proof/CTA links, fixed/changed dimensions and rejected unsafe combinations. Compare meaning and risks, retaining rejected routes and reasons; distinguish concept variation from wording or aspect-ratio variants.

**Checks and failure.** Check meaningful differences and support. Do not generate a full Cartesian set by default or impose a universal count; honour exact requested counts. A route needing unavailable proof is blocked rather than illustrated as a real supported claim.

### C08. design-concept

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 4 territory/hypothesis; Stage 5 P01–P03.

**Inputs.** Selected territory or explicitly authorised alternatives, brief, claim/proof, action, context and the decision to be resolved by the representation.

**Operation and outputs.** Write an actionable concept: opening, communication device, beats/composition, proof treatment, intended takeaway and action. Produce the cheapest actual text/layout/sequence representation required for that decision, with alternatives, selection rationale and fixed/open elements.

**Checks and failure.** Inspect coherence, implied meaning and medium feasibility. Preference-for-review is not a market winner. Creative approval belongs to its actual authority. Escalate missing story-changing or business facts when required; a treatment is not a finished video or image.

### C09. build-creative-brief

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 5 P03–P06; Stage 7 request/return; Stage 12 Q01–Q08.

**Inputs.** Accepted concept/master, exact claims/qualifiers and brand/source assets, requested format/placement/fidelity, specialist responsibility and actual resource authority.

**Operation and outputs.** Produce the specialist request with exact inputs, fixed/changeable dimensions, modality/script/visual/audio direction as relevant, current profile, output/provenance and independent acceptance criteria. Include cost/attempt, data-recipient and failure boundaries; name missing prerequisites.

**Checks and failure.** Check that the request is actionable without granting new rights or spending permission. When returned work is part of the task, inspect actual files and receipts. A completed brief alone does not complete a request for finished media; absent required identity or authority blocks production.

### C10. adapt-placement

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 5 six-layer lineage and nine formats; Stage 6 M02; Stage 7.

**Inputs.** Exact selected master/format/asset revision, target placement/product/profile and interaction/modality, locale, claims, qualification and action constraints.

**Operation and outputs.** Re-express meaning for the placement: composition, sequencing, field assembly, timing, audible/visible information and feasible CTA. Record fixed/changed elements and exact adapted text or actual required specialist output. Preserve production parents and separate evidential lineage.

**Checks and failure.** Review the actual requested fidelity, necessary conditions, safe assembly and destination continuity. Do not solve space pressure by removing material qualifiers or changing terms. Unsupported interaction/profile blocks that route; repair only the affected adaptation and recheck dependencies.

### C11. build-media-plan

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 6 M01–M11/B01; Stage 3 commercial boundary.

**Inputs.** Approved channel class, objective/audience, commercial market, budget/economics/schedule and creative/destination requirements; current profile evidence where selected.

**Operation and outputs.** Produce versioned media lines covering all eleven media dimensions, role rationale, test allocation, exclusions and suitability. Reconcile included costs/reserve; distinguish forecast, configuration, actual spend and disjoint remaining liabilities/executable exposure. Specify bounded external execution requests.

**Checks and failure.** Check creative-context fit, units/timezones, hard-rule enforcement and the authorised envelope. Forecasts are not outcomes; daily settings are not assumed hard ceilings. Infeasible control or new commercial channel scope returns to its owner, not an autonomous spend action.

### C12. design-test

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stage 8 T01–T09; Stage 6 allocation.

**Inputs.** Decision question, exact proposed treatments/context, available assignment/observation mechanisms, primary and downstream metric definitions, horizon and resource boundaries.

**Operation and outputs.** Define hypothesis, changed variables, comparison, primary metric, guardrails, downstream business metric, evidence threshold, confounders and ordered decision rule. Specify assignment/counting units, analysis/maturation windows, validity diagnostics, stopping and estimator requirements appropriate to the contrast.

**Checks and failure.** Check feasibility and causal scope, including multiple changes, interference, missingness and immature outcomes. No fixed universal sample or significance threshold. An infeasible confirmatory question is explicitly unresolved or exploratory only with an authorised scope change; design acceptance does not run the test.

### C13. prepare-launch-package

**Owner:** `advertising-build`. **Model authority:** Stages 5–8/10/12; original production chain.

**Inputs.** Same-revision brief/strategy, actual required assets/components, media/targeting configuration requirements, destination/event evidence, applicable reviews and exact intended launch scope.

**Operation and outputs.** Assemble an inspectable manifest linking accepted outputs, placement mappings, commercial/claim/disclosure dependencies, measurement/test contract, validity windows, authorities, blockers and ad-operations request. Reconcile all returns against one baseline and state readiness separately for each gate.

**Checks and failure.** Fail or block the affected readiness claim when assets, journey, tracking, hard constraints or required decisions are absent. An audit can be complete while the package is not launch-ready. No upload, activation, serving, purchase or spend is performed merely by assembling the package.

### C14. ingest-results

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 8 observation contract and reconciliation; Stage 9 L05.

**Inputs.** Permitted source snapshots/exports or authorised exact reads, source owners, test/variant/context identities and event/metric/window definitions.

**Operation and outputs.** Create source-separated observation and reconciliation records. Preserve raw or permitted immutable references, numerator/denominator, counting unit, currency/value, time, maturity, modelled/restricted/unknown status and technical checks. Record explained differences and unresolved residuals.

**Checks and failure.** Check qualification, duplicates, environment, population and comparable windows before totals or ratios. Do not add incompatible platform credits or fill missing values with zero. Route data defects to their owner; actual business verification and causal attribution are separate conclusions.

### C15. diagnose-delivery

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 9 delivery layer; Stage 6; Stage 12 X03–X05.

**Inputs.** Approved media/asset baseline, actual configuration/delivery observations, change history, relevant reporting validity and exact diagnostic scope.

**Operation and outputs.** Compare intended and observed assignment, activation, schedule, inventory and serving. Distinguish absent evidence from no delivery, technical errors from low response, and enabled state from measured exposure. Return supported findings, alternatives, affected period and bounded ad-operations request.

**Checks and failure.** Verify source scope and actual return before claiming a stop or fix occurred. A broken upload is not a failed concept. Unresolved external state requires operator/read-back evidence; do not repair it by rewriting creative or silently activating an account.

### C16. diagnose-creative

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 9 creative layer; Stages 4/5/8.

**Inputs.** Actual expression/output, accepted message/claim/proof/master, placement context and valid observations where performance diagnosis is requested.

**Operation and outputs.** Inspect whether the delivered opening, hierarchy, proof treatment, sequence and CTA communicate the intended supported meaning. Separate a demonstrable craft/semantic defect from a hypothesis about response. Return implicated elements, evidence, alternatives and smallest repair brief.

**Checks and failure.** Inspect actual required media, not only its prompt or thumbnail. Low performance with invalid tracking does not prove weak creative. Preserve sound claims and unaffected siblings; a new communication mechanism is a versioned new concept, not a concealed local edit.

### C17. diagnose-audience

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 3 A01–A03; Stage 6 M03/M10; Stage 9.

**Inputs.** Approved segment, audience hypothesis and representation, actual permitted delivery/qualification evidence, exclusion/suppression status and comparable result definitions.

**Operation and outputs.** Determine whether evidence challenges the hypothesis, exposes a representation/expansion fault, or reflects qualification/measurement failure. Retain uncertainty and population limits. Return a bounded hypothesis revision or executor/data-owner repair with explicit commercial constraints.

**Checks and failure.** Do not infer sensitive traits, reconstruct people or redefine the customer from platform categories. Missing suppression or signal-only constraints block dependent actions. Commercial segment/eligibility changes remain upstream even when a different audience appears cheaper.

### C18. diagnose-placement

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 5 P04/P05; Stage 6 M02; Stage 9.

**Inputs.** Actual placement/assembly context and profile, delivered variant, media role, interaction/destination requirements and valid comparable observations.

**Operation and outputs.** Assess format fit, viewing/listening conditions, inventory, composition, assembly and allocation against the intended role. Separate a local placement mismatch from master/concept failure. Return affected variant/line, supporting evidence and an eligible bounded change.

**Checks and failure.** Check context-specific claims/qualifications and available actions. Aggregate performance or a recommendation is not a hard product constraint. Preserve valid parent meaning; a new provider/context needs its own profile and authority review before execution.

### C19. diagnose-destination

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 7 D01–D07; Stage 9 destination layer.

**Inputs.** Exact entry ad/CTA and approved terms/proof, actual relevant journey states/version, event definitions and permitted technical observations.

**Operation and outputs.** Trace message, price, claim, proof, CTA, tracking and disclosures through relevant branches, including failure and commitment states. Return a dimension-specific mismatch, its owner, affected observations and the smallest UIUX/Software/business/Legal handoff.

**Checks and failure.** A reachable URL, screenshot or successful event transport is insufficient for every branch. Do not buy, submit a form or publish reports merely to inspect. Keep a tracker repair separate from copy and preserve invalid historical measurement with its limitation.

### C20. detect-fatigue

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 9 F1–F6; Stage 8 validity.

**Inputs.** Parent/variant exposure history, defined audience/placement/time context, comparable mature outcomes, declared deterioration criterion and competing-cause evidence.

**Operation and outputs.** Examine signal, evidence health, exposure/context, alternatives, diagnosis strength and bounded response. Return not-assessed, suspected, fatigued or not-supported with the exact scope, limitations and evidence needed for a stronger conclusion.

**Checks and failure.** Age, a frequency average, low rating or aggregate decline alone cannot establish fatigue. Distinguish operational diagnosis from causally isolated repetition effects. Keep suspected when evidence is insufficient; preserve historical winners and do not invent an exposure reset from a new asset ID.

### C21. propose-next-test

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 8 T01–T09; Stage 9 learning.

**Inputs.** Current bounded decision, reconciled observations/validity, hypotheses already tested or untested, constraints, capacity and remaining authorised resources.

**Operation and outputs.** Select the smallest informative next contrast, explain alternatives and apply the complete design-test criteria while fulfilling this optimise-owned intent. Build assistance is optional, not an absent-sibling dependency. Return all nine fields, fixed/changed elements, feasibility, expected discriminating evidence and permitted next decision.

**Checks and failure.** Do not extend a fixed-horizon test until significance or rank point estimates into winners. Inconclusive, invalid and unsupported remain distinct. No reserve release, live study or changed commercial objective is authorised by the proposed next test.

### C22. refresh-creative

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 9 correction; Stage 5 production; Stage 12.

**Inputs.** Accepted diagnosis/repair scope or authorised exploratory refresh, parent concept/variant, fixed approved elements, desired fidelity and valid production authority.

**Operation and outputs.** Specify the smallest correction, route actual creation through build or the required specialist, and obtain actual replacement outputs when requested. Record before/after revisions, changed/fixed dimensions, affected claims/approvals, evaluation and adoption scope.

**Checks and failure.** Verify preservation and all dependent meaning/profile/journey checks. A request is not a finished replacement or supersession. Missing required assets/authority blocks production; preserve successful siblings and reconcile unknown billable outcomes before retrying.

### C23. preserve-learning

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`. **Model authority:** Stage 9 L01–L07 and five facets.

**Inputs.** Exact concept/variants/context, source observations, validity and uncertainty, decision/repair/adoption evidence and permitted retention rules.

**Operation and outputs.** Write a contextual learning record separating observation, explanation, decision and generalisation. Record changed elements, rejected or untested routes and review triggers. Update only supported delivery, performance, validity, fatigue and succession facets using attributable events.

**Checks and failure.** Do not call an invalid comparison a loser or a preferred draft a winner. Withdraw invalidated conclusions without deleting their history; respect lawful retention limits. Actual pause/activation/supersession needs its own evidence, not merely this record.

**Checks and failure.** Inspect actual requested output; do not equate softened wording with support. A supported price claim cannot support a fabricated outcome. Record FAIL for an unsupported assertion and BLOCKED for required inaccessible evidence, without inventing Legal approval.

### C25. audit-proof

**Checks and failure.** A source label, testimonial permission, duplicate report or generated screenshot does not establish adequacy. A promised resource must be available to the intended audience. Separate hard profile constraints, recommendations and unknown capabilities; return coverage and local repair findings.

**Checks and failure.** Same URL or a high automated page score is not semantic continuity. Missing checkout, consent/eligibility branch or event evidence is explicit. Do not silently label a design-only audit a deployed-journey pass or perform unapproved transactions.

### C28. audit-test-validity

**Checks and failure.** Do not rebalance outcomes to fix assignment problems, choose a new primary after results, or treat nonsignificance as equivalence. Unresolved material assignment/logging defects block causal claims; preserve the observations and route technical or study repair.

## 7. Routing, evaluation and authoring boundaries

Creation and verification can run in the same host, but the criteria must not be altered to match the output. A build self-check uses the same accepted evaluation semantics as an explicit audit. Separate review workers are optional, not a claim of independent human approval. A requested broad review selects all materially relevant dimensions; a narrow audit names omissions rather than silently claiming full campaign acceptance.

The nine evaluator intents do not reduce the twelve original evaluation dimensions. Claim/proof and policy cover factual and required-authority questions; format/destination cover production and continuity; test/measurement cover study quality; preservation and failure triage cover changes, campaign reasoning and business alignment. Creative quality, optimisation/fatigue, pack behaviour, end-to-end production and installation each retain their own criteria/evidence even when the active command coordinates several dimensions. Stage 18 must design their actual fixtures/rubrics; no universal score or “all tests passed” result is supplied here.

For ambiguous natural language, first use the existing subject and requested effect. “Review this ad” is evaluate, not permission to replace it. “Improve this ad” with a bounded copy-edit request can be build using fixed facts; “improve this campaign's results” is optimise and needs actual context/evidence for performance conclusions. “Make a Google pack” first tests whether a genuine campaign grammar exists; a platform requirement belongs to a profile. If ambiguity materially changes authority, deliverable or commitment, ask the owner rather than guess. Internal routing is not presented as completed delegation.

For pack use, resolve actual core compatibility, selected grammar and explicit requirements before applying defaults. Preserve verified legal/standards/platform constraints and explicit campaign requirements, then approved offer/audience/brand decisions, then selected pack, then core defaults. Conflicts at a higher level are escalated, not solved by choosing the convenient or merely newest rule. Pack defaults cannot supply real facts, source evidence, approvals, outcomes or permission. Pack authoring and provider profiles are separate artefacts; Stage 15 decides catalogue details without changing this precedence.
