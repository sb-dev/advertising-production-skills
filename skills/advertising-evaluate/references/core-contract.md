# Evaluation core contract

## Subject and criteria

Record the exact request, subject identity/revision, source/approval records, requested fidelity and criteria. A technically valid file can still fail meaning, claims, destination or experiment validity.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer direct deterministic evidence for objective properties, extracted evidence for media/data, then independent semantic judgement where needed. State blocked or omitted coverage.

## Claims and proof

Evaluate explicit and implied assertions, compound/visual implications, qualifiers, comparator/population, source validity and rights. A generated output or platform acceptance is not substantiation.

## Media and destination

Inspect actual requested media and every material assembly/state required by the task. Same URL does not prove journey continuity; a screenshot does not prove error/recovery or tracking behaviour.

## Experiment and measurement

Inspect all nine test fields, assignment/analysis/counting units, event meaning, maturity, deduplication, windows, missingness, confounders and decision rule. Nonsignificance does not establish equivalence; attribution does not establish causality.

## Policy context

Record jurisdiction, advertising standard/source, platform, category, claim type, targeting method, retrieved/effective/valid-as-of dates and campaign implication. This skill does not issue Legal conclusions.

## Preservation

Compare before/after at the property required: byte identity where exact identity matters and semantic invariants where meaning matters. A checksum proves identity, not meaning.
