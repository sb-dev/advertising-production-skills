# verify-preservation

**Owner:** `advertising-evaluate`

## Purpose
Verify that a bounded change preserved required siblings and approved semantics.

## Inputs
Exact before/after, declared repair, fixed elements, evidence/approval dependencies and unaffected siblings.

## Outputs
Intended changes, unexpected drift, preserved elements, affected approvals and dependent rechecks.

## Checks
Use byte identity only when exact identity matters; fluent summaries cannot hide altered commercial terms or claim scope.

## Failure routing
Restore drift or obtain the owning decision, then re-evaluate only the affected scope.
