# audit-proof

**Owner:** `advertising-evaluate`

## Purpose
Audit whether each material claim has adequate scoped support and usable evidence.

## Inputs
Claim/proof map, exact evidence, population/comparator/date, qualifiers, use rights and contradictions.

## Outputs
Adequate/partial/incompatible/unverified/obsolete support findings with precise scope and unresolved evidence needs.

## Checks
Several repeated secondary statements are not independent corroboration. A mock chart, generated testimonial or internal assertion is not automatically proof.

## Failure routing
Block or narrow only the unsupported claim/use while preserving supported siblings.
