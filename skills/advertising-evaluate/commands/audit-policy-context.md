# audit-policy-context

**Owner:** `advertising-evaluate`

## Purpose
Audit whether material advertising-policy/legal context is current, scoped and implemented as required.

## Inputs
Exact campaign/claim/proof facts, jurisdiction/category/targeting, source passages/dates and specialist decisions where supplied.

## Outputs
Ten-field context review, source validity/limitations, changed subjects and exact-fact questions for the appropriate Legal/policy owner.

## Checks
Future-effective, replaced or stale material sources cannot clear current use. Platform acceptance is not Legal, commercial or spend authority.

## Failure routing
Keep affected use blocked until current scoped evidence is obtained; this audit does not issue a legal conclusion.
