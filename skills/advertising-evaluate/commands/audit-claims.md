# audit-claims

**Owner:** `advertising-evaluate`

## Purpose
Audit material explicit and implied advertising assertions against the approved claim scope.

## Inputs
Exact expression/output, claim inventory, commercial/product revision, context and applicable constraints.

## Outputs
Criterion-level findings for wording, visual/temporal implication, qualification and consistency, with severity/evidence/repair owner.

## Checks
Do not accept fabricated testimonials, statistics, scarcity, guarantees, hidden terms or unsupported comparison merely because presentation is persuasive.

## Failure routing
Narrow/remove the expression or return the missing claim decision/evidence to its owner; do not invent Legal approval.
