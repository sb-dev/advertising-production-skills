# audit-format-fit

**Owner:** `advertising-evaluate`

## Purpose
Evaluate technical/contextual fit and preserved meaning in a placement.

## Inputs
Actual asset/component set, exact profile/assemblies, modality/interaction, locale and accessibility requirements.

## Outputs
Findings on technical fit, message/claim/qualification/CTA preservation and actual inspection coverage.

## Checks
One preview is insufficient when multiple assemblies are possible. Missing media or control evidence blocks its conclusion.

## Failure routing
Repair the local layout/format layer without discarding a valid master or material terms.
