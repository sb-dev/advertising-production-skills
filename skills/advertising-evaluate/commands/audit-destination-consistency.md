# audit-destination-consistency

**Owner:** `advertising-evaluate`

## Purpose
Audit ad-to-destination continuity across the relevant states.

## Inputs
Exact entry creative/action, destination content/configuration, authoritative terms/claims and branch-specific evidence.

## Outputs
Seven-dimension continuity findings, branch coverage and separated design/implementation/tracking/readiness issues.

## Checks
Same URL or a page score does not prove offer, price, claim, proof, CTA, tracking or disclosure continuity.

## Failure routing
Return each defect to the owning layer; no deployed-journey pass from a design-only representation.
