# build-claim-proof-map

**Owner:** `advertising-build`

## Purpose
Map every material explicit or implied assertion to scoped support.

## Inputs
Assertions, product/offer revision, evidence, qualifiers, rights/access and existing decisions.

## Outputs
Claim/proof inventory with adequate, partial, incompatible, unverified or obsolete support state and permitted presentation.

## Checks
Inspect compound, visual and temporal implications, population/comparator/date and rights. Generated testimonials or mock charts are not proof.

## Failure routing
Narrow/remove, block or obtain support without inventing Legal clearance.
