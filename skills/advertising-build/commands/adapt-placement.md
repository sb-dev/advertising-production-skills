# adapt-placement

**Owner:** `advertising-build`

## Purpose
Re-express an accepted master for a specific placement without losing material meaning.

## Inputs
Selected master/asset, target profile, locale, modality/interaction and claim/qualification/action constraints.

## Outputs
Exact adapted content or required actual output, parent lineage and fixed/changed comparison.

## Checks
Inspect essential conditions and allowed assemblies; do not remove material terms merely to fit space.

## Failure routing
An unsupported interaction/control blocks that route, not valid sibling placements.
