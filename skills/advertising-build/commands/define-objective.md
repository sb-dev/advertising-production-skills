# define-objective

**Owner:** `advertising-build`

## Purpose
Define the advertising-induced change and its relationship to downstream value.

## Inputs
Business outcome, economics/capacity, audience/horizon and available event/study definitions.

## Outputs
Advertising objective, primary indicator, mechanism, proxy gap, qualified downstream relationship and measurement requirements.

## Checks
State population, unit and horizon. Do not invent targets, profitable scale or platform eligibility.

## Failure routing
Missing economics or measurement implementation remains an explicit dependency, not a fabricated metric.
