# design-concept

**Owner:** `advertising-build`

## Purpose
Turn a selected territory or authorised alternatives into an actionable advertising concept.

## Inputs
Territory, brief, claim/proof state, action/context and the decision to resolve.

## Outputs
Opening, device, beats/composition, proof treatment, takeaway, CTA and cheapest useful representation; comparisons when alternatives matter.

## Checks
Inspect implied meaning and feasibility. Preference for review is not approval or a measured winner.

## Failure routing
A written treatment cannot satisfy a requested finished-media output; return the missing fidelity explicitly.
