# model-audience

**Owner:** `advertising-build`

## Purpose
Separate approved customer scope from an advertising hypothesis and executable targeting representation.

## Inputs
Approved segment, evidence/limits, intended context, exclusions and actual execution profile when selected.

## Outputs
Three distinct audience layers plus support, contrary evidence and enforcement state.

## Checks
Verify purpose, recipients, expansion and hard exclusions. Do not broaden the customer or infer sensitive traits.

## Failure routing
An unverified hard restriction blocks executable targeting while leaving unrelated campaign work intact.
