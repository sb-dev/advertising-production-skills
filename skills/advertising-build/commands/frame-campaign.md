# frame-campaign

**Owner:** `advertising-build`

## Purpose
Frame the advertising problem before production.

## Inputs
Requested advertising change, available project context, desired decision and fidelity.

## Outputs
A versioned frame covering business objective, advertising objective, audience layers, offer, evidence, brand, schedule, budget/economics, destination, measurement, claim/proof, authority and unresolved dependencies.

## Checks
Do not invent price, market, customer or approval. Unknown dependencies may be recorded but cannot silently become launch-ready facts.

## Failure routing
Return out-of-domain commercial decisions or required missing owner decisions to their owner while preserving the valid frame.
