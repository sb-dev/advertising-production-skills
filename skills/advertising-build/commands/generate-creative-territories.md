# generate-creative-territories

**Owner:** `advertising-build`

## Purpose
Explore genuinely different campaign mechanisms before polishing one expression.

## Inputs
Message/claim/proof/CTA, audience/context, brand and requested breadth.

## Outputs
Distinct treatments with mechanism, opening, proof dependency, CTA, cheap representation, fixed/changeable elements and rejected unsafe combinations.

## Checks
Do not substitute cosmetic reskins for concept alternatives or create a Cartesian set without need.

## Failure routing
A territory requiring unavailable proof stays blocked rather than being illustrated as fact.
