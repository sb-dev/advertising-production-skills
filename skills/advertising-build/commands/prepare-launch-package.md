# prepare-launch-package

**Owner:** `advertising-build`

## Purpose
Bind accepted campaign artefacts into an offline launch package without performing launch actions.

## Inputs
Same-revision brief/strategy, actual required assets, media/targeting controls, destination/event evidence, reviews and exact launch scope.

## Outputs
Manifest of actual outputs, placement mappings, claims/disclosures/terms, tests/measurement, authorities, blockers and ad-operations requests.

## Checks
Missing assets, journey, tracking, hard controls or decisions block their specific readiness gate.

## Failure routing
Package assembly cannot upload, activate, serve, buy or spend; return unresolved gates to their owner.
