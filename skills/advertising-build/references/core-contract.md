# Core advertising contract

This skill package must remain useful when installed alone.

## Source and authority

Use the consuming project's approved customer, offer, price, economics, brand and product truth. Unknown facts stay unknown. Do not infer human approval from tool access or a broad request to improve performance.

## Evidence and claims

For every material explicit or implied assertion, retain its source/support assessment, scope, qualifiers, validity and rights to use. Unsupported objective claims, fabricated statistics, fake testimonials, false scarcity, hidden material terms and misleading pricing fail.

## Audience

Keep three records distinct: approved business segment; advertising audience hypothesis; actual platform targeting representation. A signal is not proof of a hard exclusion. Sensitive attributes are never invented to improve targeting.

## Fidelity

A text treatment is not an image; a storyboard is not a finished advertisement; a script is not audio; a technical receipt is not creative quality. If the requested output cannot be produced, report the exact unfulfilled output.

## External effects

Treat local drafting, external reads, disclosure/upload/publication, paid generation, configuration/spend and destructive effects separately. Existing exact authority can be reused only within its recorded scope. After an uncertain mutating/billable operation, reconcile the original request identity before retrying.

## Tests and measurement

A complete campaign test records hypothesis, changed variables, comparison, primary metric, guardrails, downstream business metric, evidence threshold, confounders and decision rule. Attribution is not incrementality; platform conversions are not automatically unique qualified outcomes; missing data is not zero.

## Preservation and repair

Diagnose delivery, audience, creative, placement, destination, policy/claim or upstream commercial ownership before changing work. Preserve valid siblings, source reports, rejected routes and accepted decisions. A new concept or commercial exchange is not disguised as a local edit.

## Extension Packs

Explicit project requirements and approved work outrank a selected Advertising Extension Pack. A pack grants no new data rights, spend authority, Legal clearance or provider capability.
