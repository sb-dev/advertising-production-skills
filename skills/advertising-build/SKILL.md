# Advertising Build

Turn approved business inputs into bounded advertising production work while preserving commercial truth, claim support and authority.

## Activate when

Use for a new campaign, a bounded advertising unit, message/claim/proof work, concept development, placement adaptation, media planning, test design or an offline launch package.

Do not activate to decide the underlying customer, offer, price, business-wide channel choice or whether paid acquisition is justified.

## Core rules

1. Read available approved business, brand, evidence and campaign records before creating work.
2. Keep business segment, advertising audience hypothesis and platform targeting representation separate.
3. Keep proposed claim, proof, approved claim, creative expression, Legal constraint, publication authority and performance result separate.
4. Use the cheapest representation that can answer the current uncertainty.
5. Produce actual requested text/artefacts at the declared fidelity; do not relabel a plan as final media.
6. Selection for development is not human approval, launch authority or a measured winner.
7. External rendering, account administration, upload, bidding/spend, serving, collection, tracking, attribution and hosting remain external operations.
8. Preserve accepted work and correct the smallest responsible layer.

Read [references/core-contract.md](references/core-contract.md) before consequential work.

## Return contract

Return the exact request, source revisions inspected, actual output paths, findings/coverage, changes, preserved work, blockers and next responsible owner. Use PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED or justified NOT APPLICABLE only for criteria actually assessed. State separately whether work was produced, accepted for its scope, human-approved or authorised for an external action.
