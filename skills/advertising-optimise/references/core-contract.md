# Optimisation core contract

## Evidence first

Preserve raw/source references, metric definitions, numerator/denominator, units, currency/value, time zone/window, cohort maturity, modelled/restricted state and unknowns. Missing is not zero. Duplicate reporting and ineligible outcomes are distinct defects.

## Causality

Platform credit does not establish incrementality. A before/after movement does not prove one changed variable caused it when delivery mix, auction, audience, destination, seasonality or measurement changed.

## Failure layers

Diagnose delivery, audience, creative, placement, destination, claims/policy and upstream business separately. A downstream failure does not automatically reopen approved upstream work.

## Fatigue

Track parent concept, material variant, context and exposure evidence. Calendar age, frequency labels or asset renaming alone do not prove or reset fatigue.

## Repair

State the exact defect and evidence, fixed elements, smallest permitted change, actual before/after and dependent rechecks. Keep historical evidence and rejected routes. External configuration changes require their own authority and verified return.

## Test design

A next test must include hypothesis, changed variables, comparison, primary metric, guardrails, downstream metric, evidence threshold, confounders and decision rule. A designed test is not an executed test.
