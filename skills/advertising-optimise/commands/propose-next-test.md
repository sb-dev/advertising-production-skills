# propose-next-test

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`

## Purpose
Turn an unresolved diagnosis into the smallest useful next experiment.

## Inputs
Decision question, current evidence/uncertainty, feasible treatments, guardrails, downstream metric and constraints.

## Outputs
Complete nine-field test, fixed/changed dimensions, assignment/counting/analysis units, maturity and inconclusive rule.

## Checks
Do not design around a preferred answer, invent power, or change several unrelated variables when the decision requires attribution.

## Failure routing
If feasibility or authority is absent, preserve the design and mark live execution blocked.
