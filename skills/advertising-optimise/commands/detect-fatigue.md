# detect-fatigue

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`

## Purpose
Assess whether evidence supports creative fatigue rather than another delivery/context change.

## Inputs
Concept/variant lineage, material changes, exposure context, comparable results, frequency/reach evidence and validity limits.

## Outputs
Scoped fatigue state with supporting/contrary evidence, alternatives, affected context and next discriminating action.

## Checks
Calendar age, a platform frequency label or asset renaming alone is insufficient. Separate aggregate mix shifts from within-context change.

## Failure routing
Use suspected/not-supported/blocked where appropriate; do not manufacture a permanent winner/loser state.
