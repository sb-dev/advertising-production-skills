# diagnose-destination

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`

## Purpose
Find continuity or implementation defects after the advertisement handoff.

## Inputs
Exact advertisement/action, authoritative commercial/claim sources, destination state/branches, tracking evidence and actual observations.

## Outputs
Seven-dimension continuity findings, branch gaps, implementation/measurement distinctions and smallest repair request.

## Checks
The newest page is not automatically authoritative. A screenshot cannot prove interactive states or successful tracking.

## Failure routing
Repair destination-owned defects without rewriting an accepted advertisement unless evidence shows the ad itself failed.
