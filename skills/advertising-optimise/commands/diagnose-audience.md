# diagnose-audience

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`

## Purpose
Distinguish a challenged audience hypothesis from a targeting/configuration or qualification defect.

## Inputs
Approved segment, audience hypothesis/representation, permitted delivery/qualification evidence and exclusions.

## Outputs
Supported audience findings, representation/control findings, bounded hypothesis revision or owner repair request.

## Checks
Do not reconstruct people, infer sensitive traits or redefine the customer from platform categories.

## Failure routing
Missing suppression or signal-only hard controls block dependent actions; cheaper response does not authorise new commercial scope.
