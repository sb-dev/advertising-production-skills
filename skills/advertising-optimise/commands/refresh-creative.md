# refresh-creative

**Owner:** `advertising-optimise`

## Purpose
Produce a bounded creative refresh only after the responsible layer and preserved meaning are known.

## Inputs
Exact parent concept/asset, supported defect or refresh objective, fixed claims/offer/brand, context and required fidelity.

## Outputs
Actual requested refreshed expression, lineage, declared changes, preserved elements and dependent evaluation request.

## Checks
Do not use a refresh to change offer/customer/proof or to reset exposure history by renaming an asset.

## Failure routing
A new mechanism routes to concept work; missing final-media execution remains an unfulfilled output.
